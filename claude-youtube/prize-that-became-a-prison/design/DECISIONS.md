# The Prize That Became a Prison: decisions

Settled with the author. Started 2026-11-26.

| Decision | Choice |
| --- | --- |
| Script | The author's locked narration of 5 November 2026: 2,015 words, 12 chapters, about 15:28, with visual, sound and fact-check notes (`design/script/Dawn_Hunt_Script.pdf`). |
| Style references | The two films the author supplied on 26 November 2026, studied in `design/REFERENCES.md`. |
| Look | **Cinematic investigative noir**: stylized 3D plus archival collage. Lighting desaturated blue, fluorescent airport light, deep shadows. Camera: slow tracking shots, overhead compositions, controlled close-ups. The author, 26 November 2026. |
| Characters | Realistic proportions, simplified matte faces (first faceless; now natural faces kept sculptural, see Character asset), body language carries the emotion. **One character system shared with the author's other documentary**; the two are told apart by lighting, composition, motion graphics and sound, so the channel has one recognisable look and the work scales. |
| Scene presentation | **Fluorescent Noir 50 %, Evidence Table 30 %, Void Stage 20 %** of the running time (scene presentation, separate from the 3D / graphics / archive production mix). Fluorescent Noir is the foundation; evidence is introduced on the table; the Void Stage is kept for moments such as the death sentence and the passing of years in prison. The author, 26 November 2026, after the three-option boards. Chapter by chapter: `design/STYLE_PLAN.md`. |
| Character asset | **MakeHuman bodies (MPFB 2, CC0) with their natural faces, finished as matte statues**: one stone finish, blank statue eyes, sculpted matte hair (Dawn: drawn back into a low bun; the men: short), no realistic skin or hair, faces generic and never modelled on a real person, so the picture itself reads as reconstruction. The author, 26 November 2026, after comparing them with the rigged human structure and with smoothed mannequin faces. Built by `noir-kit/cast.py`; posed through Rigify like every figure. |
| Outfits | **MakeHuman's own clothes** (CC0 system pack), made for these bodies, so they fit and follow the rig by design (the author, 26 November 2026, after fitted Sketchfab garments looked wrong). Dawn 2014: female elegantsuit01 (blouse and skirt), muted. Dawn in prison: female casualsuit01 in flat grey (no logo). Officers: male elegantsuit01 in navy, with the peaked cap (licence to confirm). Tim at Congress: male elegantsuit01 in charcoal. Gene and the trip's organisers: male casualsuit05 / 01 / 03, muted. Recolours keep the weave and drop print colours; garments with logos take a flat colour. No credits needed. Refined after the stage critiques (26 November 2026): all main garments are tinted flat (the pinstripe shimmered in wide shots), everyone wears plain black shoes (shoes04, officers shoes03 boots; the sneaker models read as brands), officers wear the Tripo peaked cap, and the three officers differ in height, build and age. Garments sit well below the stone skin in value (Dawn 2014 in dark umber, Dawn in prison in charcoal grey, about two stops under the skin even under a hard top light), because a mid taupe read as bare skin to the critic. Fabric is written as the colour it should read and rendered at 0.4 of it with low specular (0.2) and roughness 0.85, because next to the stone skin a 0.09 garment showed as mid grey and Blender's default specular greyed even black cloth (measured 26 November 2026). Built once in `scenes/people.py` so every scene matches. Refinements made in the shared kit by Codex during the two-agent shift of 27 November 2026: Dawn's 2014 shoes are plain black leather flats with bare ankles (the stock shoe had a stamped toe and sock cuffs); the bright bars on both sides of her suitcase are covered with plain nylon; Tim's suit takes separate jacket, shirt and tie tones; and a long-sleeved prison uniform with a collar and straight trousers exists (`people.dawn_prison(uniform=True)`). **Prison scenes use the long-sleeved uniform everywhere** (courtroom, Void Stage, visiting room): the T-shirt and leggings read as gym clothes, and one outfit keeps continuity. Set by the lead session on 27 November 2026 after comparing the two side by side; the author can reverse it. The Sketchfab garments and `noir-kit/fit.py` stay as a fallback only. |
| Tools | Blender 5.3 (headless `bpy`) for 3D; motion graphics as HTML canvas `draw(t)` pages rendered with Playwright; real footage and images composited in. Method from anyscene, anyshot, anymachine, anywreck, anykit and cinetic (the author's references, 26 November 2026). |
| Environments | Realistic airports, detention corridors, courtrooms, restrained prison reconstructions. |
| Graphics | Animated flight routes, dates, documents, photographs, newspaper reports. |
| Mix | About 50 % stylized 3D, 30 % investigative motion graphics, 20 % authentic archive. |
| Ending | Quiet, not sensational: a real photograph, an empty chair and a final date carry more than an elaborate animated sequence. |
| Grade | **A fixed** (the author, 27 November 2026): grade A as approved (curves cooling the shadows, saturation 0.55, vignette, fine grain), with its vignette applied in RGB instead of limited-range YUV. As first written, the vignette drove dark edges below video black and clipped them (frame pixels below 8/255: 24 % on average over seven frames; 8.6 % fixed). B (an extra contrast S-curve) was declined. Defined once in `scenes/shoot.py`; `scenes/regrade_frames.sh` re-applies it to the raw frames. |
| On-screen labels | **None, ever.** No source credits on shots, no citations or S1–S9 codes, no "reconstruction", "AI voice" or "opinion" tags, and none of the script's own label set (FAMILY ACCOUNT, CHINESE GOVERNMENT POSITION, RECONSTRUCTION / ILLUSTRATIVE RECONSTRUCTION, UNCONFIRMED MEDICAL CONCERN, "RECREATION – NOT ACTUAL EMAIL", "POSSIBLE OVARIAN CANCER – NOT CONFIRMED", the flight-discrepancy lower third). The author, 26 November 2026: "Never add this." The narration carries the attribution; sources go in the YouTube description, as the script's last chapter says. Story typography (dates, "2014 / CHINA", "YES", "WHEN?", "DEATH SENTENCE / 2017", "9 DAYS") is not a label and stays. |
| Script version 2 | **`design/script/SCRIPT_V2.md` replaces the PDF's narration and direction** (the author, 27 November 2026: "Don't stick exactly with the pdf script but use it as inspiration and foundation... Even the direction... I want this to be high hook, engaging and intense", more cinematic than fern). The PDF stays the factual foundation: every fact, date, number and attribution in version 2 comes from it and its sources S1–S9.
