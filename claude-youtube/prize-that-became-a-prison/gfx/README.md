# Motion graphics

The film's type and documents, built in code; every frame is a function of t. Lead session's lane (DECISIONS,
27 November 2026). Motion numbers from cinetic (`design/REFERENCES.md`).

| File | What it makes |
| --- | --- |
| `type.html` + `type_render.js` | The story type: supers (`place_2014`, `date_gene`), YES landing twice (`yes`), YES eroding into WHEN? (`yes_when`), `death_2017`, the sentence changing in place to life (`sentence_life`), the rolling years (`counter`), the `title`, the other place and date supers (`place`: CHICAGO, HONG KONG / 9 DAYS top centre in the street's strip of sky, 2017, JULY over 2026; `--hash "a=2026&pre=JULY"`, `pos=tc`, `fit`, `shade=1`), and the nine chapter titles (`chapter`: the number and a hairline, then the title in italic sentence case, top left over each chapter's establishing shot; parameters in the URL hash, `--hash "n=01&title=The+winner&out=4.0"`, with `side=r` to mirror it and `out` for the letters to leave so the shot carries on clean). Three looks on the board (`design/boards/type_looks.jpg`); **B, cinema serif (Cormorant Garamond)**, is the working choice. `node gfx/type_render.js seq <mode> <s> out.mov --look B` gives an alpha layer to lay over a plate; `--bg plate.jpg` previews over it. |
| `doc_composite.py` + `shots/*.json` | A real document page pinned onto evidence_table's blank-sheet plate through its corner pins, lit by the plate (multiplied in linear light), a marker written over the lit phrase, a focus band, a log-space push, then the film's grade. Private lines named in `soft` are blurred in page space. Run with the graphics Python (numpy, OpenCV): `python gfx/doc_composite.py --shot gfx/shots/s1_yes.json --out out/gfx/s1_yes.mp4` (about 1 s a frame), or `--still 3.0 out.png`. |
| `screen.html` + `shots/screen_*.json` | The laptop screen in chapter 1: an invented, unbranded mail client (`inbox`, `email`, whose prize line cycles through the three accounts' versions) and a `search` with greeked results; rendered with the skill's `canvas_render.js --size 1500x1000`, then pinned onto evidence_table `screen` by `doc_composite.py` (a `texture` video in the shot file: the UI scales the blank display's own glow). |
| `route.html` | The route map in seconds: `leg1` (Chicago → Hong Kong, 9 DAYS), `mainland` (the solid leg in, ABOUT 9 MORE DAYS, the dashed line toward Australia), `split` (dashed Australia and Qingdao, neither resolved). The mainland is a region, never a pin. |
| `scale.html` + `shots/scale_overweight.json` | The check-in scale's passenger display: a bar fills past the limit tick, then OVERWEIGHT blinks twice and holds (no weight shown: the sources say only "slightly over"); pinned on checkin_hall `scale` with its defocus (`tex_blur_px`). |
| `seam.html` | The film's only red. `flash` (cold open, 1 s) and `trace` (ch 3) run along the tote's seam from a JSON path (Codex's projected `frames/raw/evidence_table/purses.seam.json`, REQUEST L3: 212 samples, the handle tabs' hidden runs skipped; the edge-detected prototype is kept in `shots/`); `cutaway` is a schematic section of the bag wall whose lining swings open on its stitches to an empty, red-outlined space and snaps shut. No packets, no words. Render with `type_render.js --page gfx/seam.html`. |
| `split.py` | Chapter 4's split frame: the cognac tote as a gift (evidence_table `purses`, warm) beside the same tote under the officer's hand (inspection_room `handbags`, cold), each pushed 3 %, graded together, a 4 px dark gutter; at 2.2 s the right half cuts on its tote's top seam to seam.html `cutaway_half`, which eases back to the whole section and holds it open. No headings. `out/gfx/split.mp4`. |
| `montage.py` | The cold open's flash-forward: ten shots of what is coming, 19 frames down to 12, constant-speed pushes alternating in and out, the red seam (seam.html `flash`) as the second shot; the title lands after it. `out/gfx/montage.mp4`. |
| `plate_type.py` | Story type over a living plate: the raw Blender plate pushed in slowly (log space, 2-8 %), graded with the film's grade (grain fresh each frame), the moment's own plate move from `window.plateCam` (the second YES's cut and closing light), then the type on top. Every type-over-plate shot goes through it, so none is a frozen still. `--hash` passes a moment's parameters (the chapter titles). |
| `fonts/` | OFL fonts (see its README). Cormorant's figures are frozen to lining tabular (`cormorant-lt-*`, made with opentype-feature-freezer from the fontsource files), so years roll without shifting and numerals sit on the line with capitals. |

Outputs go to `out/gfx/` (ignored).
