## Palette and rules

A small, close, modern chamber score: felt-muted upright piano, low strings (cellos and violas, a few violins high and
soft), cello pizzicato, a dark airy synth pad, and a slow low brass swell used once (the title). No drums or kit, no
vocals or choir, no risers, braams or hits (the two low impacts are sound effects). Entries come in from nothing under
the end of the shot before and rise; exits are designed (the script's hard stops, or a natural decay), never a fade
under a line that matters. Everything in tune at A = 440, measured and retuned within 5 cents. Prompts name no sound
effects, no drugs, no violence and no existing works.

## Key map

Home key **D minor**. Cold open: D minor, the pulse on D. Ch 01-02: **F major**, the relative major (warmth), with a
Lydian lift in Hong Kong. Ch 03: F major turning to D minor under the same four notes. Ch 04: D minor; after the
discovery a low D with an E-flat above. Ch 05: unscored. Ch 06: D minor thinning to a held A. Ch 07: D minor. Ch 08:
B-flat major moving to D minor. Ch 09: D minor; **the last note is A over the D drone**: an open fifth with no third,
neither major nor minor, because no one has an answer. The drone (D2 73.42 Hz, A2 110 Hz) and the low impacts (pitched
to D1) sit in the same key.

## Arc

From no music, a low D rises under the check-in; the pulse builds and stops dead on YES; silence; it rebuilds and is cut
dead on "were not."; an impact, rain and near silence; the title cue builds from nothing to the film's first peak and
decays to silence. Warmth in Chicago (piano, the prize), light motion in Hong Kong (the tick). The warmth turns cold with
the gift; the pulse returns at the belt and stops at the discovery; a cold held note. No score in the detention years:
a clock. One impact at the sentence; grave strings thinning to one note; silence. Then spare piano and long holds for
the last four minutes, silence at the strongest lines, total silence for the empty chair, and the drone with one warm
note at the end.

**Levels.** Under the voice: -14 dB (the cold-open pulse) to -24 dB (the last chapters); in voice gaps a few dB higher;
the title peak, which has no voice over it, at about the voice's own level. Ducks 9 dB where it competes with the
voice; stop-downs (the music stopping for 0.6 s) at the YES lines. Unscored: the first 2 s, the YES beats, the stretch
from "were not." to the title build, all of chapter 5, the death, the question, "It was hers." and the status line
(the full list is `unscored` in the JSON). About 536 s of the 775 s are scored (69 %), most of it very quiet.

### Purpose of each placement

- **SC00a** (0:02-0:15): After 2 s with no music, a low D appears under the scale and the hall and rises slowly, so the film is already tense before anything is said to be wrong.
- **SC00b** (0:15-0:33): The pulse starts when she is called and builds through the room; it stops dead on the first "Yes."
- **SC00c** (0:44-1:05): The linings, the prize, the drone under the void: building to "The luggage was hers. The drugs, she said, were not." and cut dead.
- **SC00d** (1:26.5-1:41): The flash-forward: no voice, the film's first peak on the title, then silence; cut on the silence to Ch 01. Carried over the montage, not cut to its beat.
- **SC01** (1:42.5-3:03.5): The warmth before: who she was, the sweepstakes, the email, the tickets. The motif is planted here.
- **SC02** (3:06-3:57): Eighteen days in motion: the tick that will become the gate.
- **SC03** (4:01-4:38.5): The gift. The same four notes over a new bass: the warmth turns cold, as YES will mean two things.
- **SC03b** (4:46-5:31): The pulse returns at the belt and runs through the gate and the room to the discovery.
- **SC04b** (5:46-6:18): Two meanings, and the doubt over the flight: cold and still under the route map.
- **SC06** (7:35-8:33.5): The sentence, the ministry, the family: grave and spare, thinning to one note.
- **SC07** (8:47-10:07.5): The wait: years, illness, letters, hearings. Spare piano; the slow pulse under the cell pull-back.
- **SC08** (10:11-10:40.5): The father. Then nothing: the total silence belongs to the empty chair.
- **SC09a** (11:12-11:38.5): Tim at the glass, the sister he barely recognises.
- **SC09b** (11:51-12:09): September 2026, the empty place, "Remember the question...". Out for "It was hers."
- **SC09c** (12:43-12:53): The one warm note: the motif's first note alone over the drone, neither major nor minor, because no one has an answer.
