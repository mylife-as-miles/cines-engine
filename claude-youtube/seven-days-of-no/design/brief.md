# Brief: What If You Said No to Everyone for 7 Days?

A POV explainer for YouTube (long-form, 16:9, about 3:38). The narrator puts "you" in a week of saying no to everyone: Monday the boss, Tuesday a friend, Wednesday Mum, Thursday a coworker, Friday a stranger, Saturday the boss again (the real answer), Sunday the tally; then the insight (most yeses are fear), the advice ("let me think and get back to you"), the wrap and a comments call.

- **Voice:** the author's recording is final and sets every timing (`audio/vo/voice_2.mp3`; the slate at 108.5–113.5 s is cut).
- **Look:** the author's references in `design/refs/` (flat cartoon, thick outlines, warm orange palette, the bald teal-hoodie hero and five supporting characters), drawn in code.
- **Deliverables:** the 1080p video with a mixed soundtrack (voice, light music, effects); captions as an `.srt`; three thumbnails; title, description and chapters.
- **Studio:** Claude YouTube, built with the Claude Film engine and method.
