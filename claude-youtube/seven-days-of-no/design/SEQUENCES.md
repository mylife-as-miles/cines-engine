# Seven days of no: the video, beat by beat

Times are on the **edited narration clock** (`out/vo/narration.wav`, 217.5 s; `tools/vo_edit.py` cuts the slate; sentence and word times in `audio/vo/words.json`). Every picture change is anchored to a spoken word, so a re-timed take re-times the video. The narrator voices every line, including the other characters' questions; a character's mouth moves lightly while the narrator speaks their words. On-screen text is allowed in this video where it names, labels or shows a screen (day cards, phone screens, three buttons), never to repeat the narration.

Sets: **office** (refs 04, 06, 09, 11), **living** (refs 03, 07; evening and morning light), **street** (ref 05), **plain** (a flat teal ground with nothing else, ref 08: the "thinking" register for the hook, the problem, the insight and the advice), **call** (split screen: the hero on one side, the caller in their own place on the other). Cast: hero, boss, friend, mum, coworker, stranger (ref 12).
