# Titles

Eight options, all under 60 characters, the hook in the first words (a phone cuts a title at about 40 to 50
characters). Each title is meant to sit beside a thumbnail that says something it does not: the title states
the premise or the claim, the thumbnail shows a consequence or a feeling. ★ marks the top three, with a
thumbnail for each (drawn as an illustrated poster from the film's own characters, 2 to 4 words at the top,
nothing in the bottom-right corner where the timestamp sits).

## Top three, with their thumbnails

**1. What If You Said No to Everyone for 7 Days?**
Thumbnail: Wednesday evening. The hero on the sofa, calm, sipping tea with a side-eye at the coffee table,
where the phone is huge, buzzing (shake lines) and lit with a red badge and "Mom". Words at the top:
**9 MISSED CALLS**. The title gives the premise; the thumbnail shows its first funny consequence and makes
the viewer want the rest of the week.

**2. What Happens When a People-Pleaser Says No for 7 Days**
Thumbnail: Monday. The hero braced in a cooking-pot helmet behind a frying-pan shield, sweat drop, while the
boss in the doorway, clipboard under his arm, just shrugs. Words at the top: **PREPARED FOR WAR**. The
title asks what happens; the thumbnail shows the anticlimax (all that dread, and a shrug), which is the
video's first answer.

**3. Most of Your Yeses Aren't Kindness**
Thumbnail: the hook image from 0:08. The hero with a wide, fixed smile and a thumbs-up, while the body
trembles (shake lines, sweat drops, knocking knees) on the plain teal ground. Words at the top:
**BODY SAYS NO**. The title makes the claim; the thumbnail is the feeling every people-pleaser knows, so
the pair reads as "this is about you".

For Test & Compare, run 1, 2 and 3 as three thumbnails under title 1, or each pair as its own test.
