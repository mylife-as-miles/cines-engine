# Titles

Eight options, all under 60 characters, with the hook in the first words (a phone cuts a title at about 40 to 50
characters). The working title is "Firstborn in an African Home". Each title is meant to sit beside a thumbnail
that says something the title does not: the title names who it is for or makes the claim, and the thumbnail
shows one picture from the video with a feeling in it. ★ marks the top three, each with a thumbnail idea
(drawn as an illustrated poster from the video's own characters, 2 to 4 words at the top, nothing in the
bottom-right corner where the timestamp sits).

## Top three, with their thumbnails

**1. POV: You're the Firstborn in an African Family**
Thumbnail: the hero bent under the whole house on his back (the picture from "this is too heavy", 5:41),
the younger siblings sitting on the roof waving, a rice pot and a ringing phone hanging off the eaves.
Words at the top: **CARRYING EVERYONE**. The title puts you in the role; the thumbnail shows what the
role weighs.

**2. Born, Hired, Promoted, Never Paid: The African Firstborn**
Thumbnail: the newborn hero swaddled in teal in the maternity cot, blinking, with the "FIRSTBORN" ID
badge on a lanyard round his neck and a job-offer card beside him, Mum and Dad beaming behind.
Words at the top: **CAN'T RESIGN**. The title lists the career; the thumbnail adds the one term of
the contract it leaves out, on a baby, which is the joke at a glance.

**3. Why the Firstborn in an African Home Is Always Tired**
Thumbnail: the grown-up hero behind a bank counter, smiling through it, a queue of the family reaching
out of the frame (Mum with a phone, a cousin with a school-fees bill, an uncle with a hospital bill).
Words at the top: **THE FAMILY BANK**. The title asks why; the thumbnail gives the funniest of the
answers and leaves the honest one for the video.

For Test & Compare, run the three thumbnails under title 1; if title tests are available on the channel,
test titles 1 and 2 with their own thumbnails.
