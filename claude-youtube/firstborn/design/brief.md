# Brief: Firstborn in an African Home

A POV explainer for YouTube (long-form, 16:9, about 6:50 with the end screen), the second in the channel's series after "What If You Said No to Everyone for 7 Days?". The narrator (a woman, off screen) walks "you", the firstborn of an African family, through your whole life: born and immediately "hired"; your parents' version 1.0; promoted to carer at six; the house rules and the family audit; a teenager whose grades belong to the family; the first salary and the family bank (black tax); management at 30 (weddings, funerals, the rice); then the honest turn (the role is position, not talent; the pressure is love said badly), one thing to share, and a call to the babies of the family to call their elder sibling.

- **Voice:** the author's recording (`audio/vo/voice.mp3`, 401.5 s) is final and sets every timing; no cuts.
- **Look:** the "What If You Said No" look and kit, unchanged: the same hero exactly as he was, as "you"; a new African family drawn in that style.
- **Tone:** warm, funny, affectionate, never mocking; the comedy is recognition, and the last third is sincere.
- **Deliverables:** the 1080p video with a mixed soundtrack (voice, music, effects); captions (.srt/.vtt); three thumbnails; titles, description, chapters, a pinned comment.
