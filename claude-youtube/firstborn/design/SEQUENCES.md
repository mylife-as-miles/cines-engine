# Firstborn in an African Home: the plan, chapter by chapter

Times are on the narration clock (`out/vo/narration.wav`, 401.5 s; words in `audio/vo/words.json`). Every shot and sound cue is anchored to a spoken word (`W('hired')`), never a typed time. The narrator voices everyone, including the family's lines; a character's mouth moves lightly while the narrator speaks their words. On-screen text only names, labels or shows a screen (job cards, a bank app, a report card, three doors), never repeats the narration. Something changes every 4–8 s; no frame holds still (breath, blinks, a slow push). The hero is "you": the same design throughout, smaller when young.

Sets (new unless noted): **maternity** (hospital room, a cot, a window), **home** (the family living room: sofa, a wall clock, family photos, a standing fan, a TV, a dining table, the door), **kitchen**, **shop** (a small corner kiosk for the salt errand), **school** (a classroom or a results board), **office** (reused), **party** (a wedding or naming-ceremony hall: canopy, chairs, a big pot of rice), **plain** (the teal ground, reused), **call** (split screen, reused), **phone screens** (reused: a bank app, calls, messages), plus graphic stages (a life timeline path, a lab, a courtroom gag, a podium).
