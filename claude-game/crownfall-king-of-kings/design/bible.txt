THE LOOK



Cold, dusty and patient
Every frame keeps a person small against something vast, and bright colour is rationed to
five things.

1     Keep a person in the frame. The coupling, the block and the ship are only as big as the
      figure beside them. No shot of scale without someone to measure it.

2     Hold the wide when it matters. At the order (5.8), the fall (7.3) and the silence (8.4) the
      camera stays back. Close shots are kept for hands, the wrist and the seal.

3     Stay at eye level. The camera stands where a person could stand. It leaves the ground
      twice only: inside the hull (9.3) and at the ward officer's glass (10.3).

4     Light comes from things we can see. A lamp, a gun, the sun, the ward. Nothing is lit to
      look beautiful.

5     Ration the colour. Slate, ash and charcoal carry the film. White, copper, silver, violet and
      red each belong to one thing.

6 The picture says it before the line does. If a shot needs its dialogue to be understood,
      restage the shot.


    THE LOOK IN ONE PARAGRAPH, FOR BRIEFS

    Pre-dawn slate and ash, then a low clean sun through dust. Wide frames at eye level, small figures
    against terraces, stone and hulls. Deep charcoal shadows that still hold detail. Light from a lamp,
    gun-flash and sunrise only. One white lamp, one violet light, blood-red banners held back until
    the last half minute. Fine grain. No glow, no lens flare.

WHAT THE ELEVEN KEYS ADD UP TO

  Cold, then burning, then gold. Scenes 1 to 5 are slate. Scene 6 is the only orange frame. From scene
  7 the film is ochre and gold.
  The brightest thing is always small. A lamp, a slit in a shutter, a mirror. Never the sky and never a
  face.
  Every key has someone in it. The smallest figure in each frame is the one the scene is about.

THE FOUR POWERS



Aethelgard and Solforiz
The two that fight, each with its own shape and its own material.

Aethelgard                   THE BLOOD-THRONE

"Blood remembers what stone forgets."



Cinder         #1B1917   Volcanic grey   #4A4541   Iron      #6E6F72   Bone       #D8CFBD   Blood red    #8E1B1B


In this film. Cael, the captain, the army, the batteries, the banners.
SCRIPT        On screen. Dented armour. A bandaged arm. Banners kept bound until the last minute.
ATLAS         Canon. Volcanic rookeries, marrow grafts, an angular alphabet cut straight into bone and
              rock.
PROPOSED      Shape. Verticals and straight cuts: poles, gun barrels, flat plate. No curves and no ornament.
PROPOSED      Materials. Black volcanic stone, dull iron, bone fittings, cloth tied down with cord.
PROPOSED      Rule. No colour until the banners open. The First-Born stay off screen in this film.




Solforiz            THE SCORCHED CALIPHATE

"May your pistons run wet and your glass stay clear."



Ash           #9A9388    Umber           #5B4636   Glass    #27343A    Brass      #A88445   Amber        #E08A3A


In this film. The capital, the Crown, Liora, the boy, the envoy, the navigator.
SCRIPT        On screen. A causeway over fused glass. Terraces, the great stair, shutters that close plate
              by plate.
ATLAS         Canon. The inland sea boiled to glass in six seconds. Ziggurat cities, plated respirator hoods,
              storm lanterns with an inverted flame, letters scratched into metal.
PROPOSED      Shape. Steps and horizontals. Everything is stacked: terrace on terrace, plate on plate.
PROPOSED      Materials. Glass underfoot, brass pipework, scorched stone, cloth the colour of ash.
PROPOSED      Rule. The city's lamps breathe on the four-beat pulse. At 6.10 they go out together.

CHOICE                        WHAT I HAVE ASSUMED


Frame shape                   2.39:1. If the film is delivered for phones, the horizontals (causeway, army, fleet) need
                              restaging for a taller frame.

Banner colour                 Blood red, from "The Blood-Throne". The script only says banners.

The breathing lamps           The city's lamps pulse in four beats and go dark at 6.10. This puts the sound motif on
                              screen. It is not in the script.

Liora's hood                  A plated respirator hood worn pushed back, from the Atlas word qazrin. The script

The medium                    The lens and grain rules assume photographed images. For animation or generated

Six or eight minutes          The keys work for both cuts. In the six-minute cut the navigator loses his two turns
