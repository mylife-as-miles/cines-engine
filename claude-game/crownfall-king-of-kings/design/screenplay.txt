                    A NOTE ON LANGUAGES

The film is spoken in Common Imperial, written here as
English.

Each nation also keeps its own tongue, and its people use it
with one another. A line marked (subtitled) is spoken in the
tongue named and subtitled with the English shown. The
spoken line follows under TRANSLATION.

    Aethelgard    Old Aethel; its battle cry is High Drakonic
    Solforiz      Kharzani
    Korvenia      High Korvenic

Native lines follow The Ash and Aether Linguistic Atlas,
Field Edition 1.0. The appendix lists each one, with its
meaning, how to say it, and whether it is quoted from the
Atlas or built from its grammar.

    FADE IN:


1   INT. KWIND-09 - FOUNDRY - NIGHT                                1

    Dark. A mechanical STROKE. Another.

    SUPER: "YEARS EARLIER."

    A copper coupling descends on chains. Nothing gives its size
    until a WORKER walks under it: four times his height.

    Soot-black hands lock the copper. Pale gloved hands close a
    silver ring around it. Violet light runs the seam.

    Behind it, more couplings wait in the dark. Rows. No one
    speaks.

                                                         CUT TO:


2   EXT. SOLFORIZ - THE FALLEN GATE - PRE-DAWN                     2

    SUPER: "SOLFORIZ. YEAR 56."

    The stroke is now a city's pulse. Four beats. Always four.

    A causeway crosses a plain of fused glass to a broken gate.
    Beyond it the capital climbs, terrace on terrace, to the
    pressureworks at its summit: THE CROWN.

    Aethelgard holds the gate and the forecourt behind it.
    Solforiz holds everything above: an inner wall, the
    terraces, the guns. Between them: abandoned cannon, and
    wounded men walking back along the causeway under a mourning
    horn.

    Over the gatehouse burns a white truce lamp. Under it the
    fighting has paused, more or less, and civilians are coming
    down the great stair. One battery in the high arcades FIRES
    anyway, to a chanted order: "Ihriq! Ihriq!"


3   EXT. GATE FORECOURT - FOOT OF THE GREAT STAIR - CONTINUOUS     3

    Where the great stair meets the forecourt stands a stone
    arch: a signal station, its mirrored storm lantern lit.

    LIORA, a young signal keeper, brings civilians down the
    damaged stair, one hand raised in the shelter sign. A small
    BOY keeps hold of her coat.
                                                                 2.


    A Solforiz shell falls short and hits the terrace above her.
    A block tears loose.

    It stops. Dust keeps falling past it. The battle thins to a
    low hum, like pressure in the ears.

    CAEL, King of Aethelgard, stands ten paces off with one hand
    open. Dented armour. An arm already bandaged. He is
    breathing too hard for a man standing still.

    He comes to her, drops to her level and takes her wrist.

                          CAEL
              Look at me. Find your feet.

    She finds them. He pulls her clear. Only then does he close
    his hand, and the block comes down where she lay.

    He stays with her a moment. Then the boy: he straightens the
    child's respirator and tests the seal with two fingers.

    A young STANDARD-BEARER has seen it all, a bound banner on
    his shoulder. He was leaving the wall. He turns back.

    Cael goes on to the gatehouse. Liora sets the boy under the
    arch, in sight of the truce lamp.

                             LIORA
                       (in Kharzani, subtitled)
              Stay.
                             TRANSLATION
              Iwqif.

    She is already turning for the stair. The rest she says to
    no one, the way it has always been said.

                          LIORA (CONT'D)
                    (under her breath)
              May the child survive in the ash.
                          TRANSLATION
              Lu=yaslam al-walad fi al-rash.

    She goes back up for the others.


4   INT. SHATTERED GATEHOUSE - CONTINUOUS                             4

    A command post in a ruin. One wall is gone. Through the
    breach: the forecourt, the arch, the boy.

    An ENVOY of Solforiz waits, immaculate. Cael's CAPTAIN
    stands by. A wounded SOLDIER reports.
                                                            3.


                      SOLDIER
          Third assault, Cyning. The inner
          batteries still hold.

                      ENVOY
          The Ash-Caliph --

                      CAEL
          He was speaking.

The envoy stops. No one has made him wait for a soldier.

                       SOLDIER
          Half the company reached the wall.
                (beat)
          The slow half came back.

Cael grips the table. It takes him a breath to answer.

                      CAEL
          Clear the road. Bring them in.

Two stretchers come in. A soldier of Aethelgard. Then a
NAVIGATOR of Solforiz, his eyes filmed like glass. The door
guard looks to Cael. Cael nods. Both are set down inside.

Under the campaign map lie three judgments, under three
different seals. Their folds have been mended many times.

                      ENVOY
          The Ash-Caliph asks for another
          council.

Cael puts a finger on one of the seals.

                      CAEL
          He wagered his crown and lost it.
          That is his seal.

                      ENVOY
          The Ash-Caliph will not live
          beneath your law.

Through the breach: the boy, small under the arch.

                      CAEL
          The same law as that boy.

                      ENVOY
          You do not own his people.

                      CAEL
          They have never been his.
                                                                4.


                           ENVOY
              Nor yours.

    No answer. Cael's hand closes on the table. He makes it
    open.

                          ENVOY (CONT'D)
              Come to the council. You would be
              safe.

                           CAEL
              Safe.

    The envoy does not say it twice.

                          CAEL (CONT'D)
              I was promised that before.

                          ENVOY
              Give him time.

                          CAEL
              I gave him years.

    The gun in the high arcade FIRES again. Dust falls on the
    judgments. The envoy shuts his eyes. It is one of his.

                          CAEL (CONT'D)
              Look what he built with them.
                    (to his captain)
              Return him to his Caliph.


5   INT. GATEHOUSE - THE BREACH - LATER                              5

    Through the breach: the envoy, small on the great stair,
    reaches the inner wall. Its gate shuts behind him.

    On his stretcher, the blind navigator turns his face up, to
    where the truce lamp hangs.

    A single shot from the terraces. The lamp bursts.

    The captain shows Cael the causeway behind them. Open.
    Empty.

                          CAPTAIN
              We can withdraw with the wounded.

    Cael looks at the boy under the arch.

                          CAEL
              And leave him to finish this?
                                                               5.


    Every gun on the terraces opens up. A strike tears into
    Aethelgard's lines. Men who were standing are not.

    Cael looks up, past the terraces, to the Crown. The captain
    follows his eyes and knows what he is about to be asked.

                          CAPTAIN
              Those works feed the batteries and
              the lower city. The stairs are
              still full.

    They are. Lamps, moving down. Liora among them.

    Cael draws breath to speak. Loses it. Steadies.

                          CAEL
              I know.

                          CAPTAIN
              Then give them time.

    The envoy's word, in his own captain's mouth. The open road
    is still behind them. Cael looks at it once.

                          CAEL
              Bring it down.

    The captain does not move. He warned him. He stayed. Now the
    order must pass through him to become fire. He turns to the
    guns.

                          CAPTAIN
                    (in Old Aethel, subtitled)
              Break the Crown!
                          TRANSLATION
              Brecath thone corune!

    Aethelgard's batteries swing up and FIRE. The
    standard-bearer reaches for the bindings of his banner. The
    captain's look stops his hand. Not yet.


6   EXT. THE CROWN / THE BREACH / THE STAIR - CONTINUOUS            6

    THE CROWN. The works take the salvo. Armoured shutters close
    over them, plate by plate. The city is protecting itself.
    The last shutter starts down across the last opening.

    It stops. The air at its edge warps. The battle thins to
    that hum again.

    THE BREACH. Cael stands in the gap, black against the
    gun-flash, bandaged arm raised, hand open. His fingers
    shake. Blood soaks the binding. He holds.
                                                                 6.


    Behind him the blind navigator sits bolt upright, his face
    to the Crown, and strikes the stretcher rail. ONE. TWO.

    THE STAIR. LIORA'S POV, from the lower flight: the king at
    the breach. That open hand. She knows what that hand does.
    She looks up. The stalled shutter.

    THREE. FOUR.

    The second salvo goes in.

    Only then does the hand come down.

    THE CROWN. The shutter drops shut. Fire shows around its
    edges, then comes through. A tower leans and falls across
    the great retaining wall. The wall gives.

    The pulse of the city stumbles. Three beats. Two.

    None.


7   EXT. SOLFORIZ - THE FALL - CONTINUOUS                             7

    Liora is already running, driving the last of them down
    ahead of her. Dust takes the flight behind her.

    She reaches the arch. The boy. A dozen others. The lantern
    lies smashed. She pulls its mirror free and keeps it.

    Above the broken wall a terrace settles. Its houses lean
    together and go into dust. The stair goes. Then the whole
    upper city comes down.

    When the dust thins, the skyline is gone.


8   EXT. FORECOURT - THE RUINS - SUNRISE                              8

    Silence on both sides. Air hisses from broken pipes.

    The men of Aethelgard do not agree on what they have seen.
    One sinks against a gun, simply glad the batteries have
    stopped. One cannot look away from the terraces. The
    standard-bearer lifts his bound banner and finds his voice.

                           STANDARD-BEARER
              We've won.

    Nobody answers. Not Cael. The standard-bearer hears the
    silence and lowers the pole.

    The silence holds. Only the pipes.
                                                                  7.


     Under the arch, grit falls from the stone. Liora looks up.

     Beneath the hiss, a PULSE. Slower than the city's. Heavier.

     The captain turns from the ruins and looks at the sky. A
     shadow slides across the army. It keeps coming.


9    EXT./INT. THE FIRST SHIP - SUNRISE                                9

     Out of the sunrise: lights, and a dark mass in the dust. The
     broken gate towers give its size.

     A keel comes clear. Then the whole vessel: lifting envelopes
     over an armoured body, Kwind-09 iron hung with Korvenian
     silver. Re-plated. Built onto, year after year.

     It keeps coming. Its engines bury every other sound.

     INSIDE THE HULL. The copper coupling from the foundry,
     turning under load. Scarred now. The same silver ring.


10   EXT. FORECOURT - THE RUINS - CONTINUOUS                           10

     The arch begins to give.

     Sunrise comes through the gaps in the ship's rigging in long
     moving bars. One crosses Liora. She puts the mirror in it
     and throws the light up at the dark belly of the ship.

     FOUR flashes. The count her city lived by.

     ABOVE, at the observation glass: a masked WARD OFFICER of
     Korvenia. Four flashes cross the mask. She opens a hand.

                           WARD OFFICER
                     (in High Korvenic, subtitled)
               Hold the stone.
                           TRANSLATION
               Va-ten-t el sveulea!

     A bell note. Violet light drops from the keel. The falling
     arch stops above their heads. The boy looks up.

     Behind the first ship, two more. Then a formation. And more.

                           CAPTAIN
               These are the forward ships.

     Under the ward, the boy finds Cael among the soldiers and
     points. Liora lowers his hand. Hers settles over her wrist.
                                                             8.


She does not look away. Across the forecourt Cael sees her.
Then the boy. Then the people standing under another power's
protection.

It goes through him. The hand at his side will not stop
shaking. He looks behind him, for the road he was offered.
It is still there. The shadow of the fleet lies along it.

                      CAPTAIN
          The stairs were full.

Cael turns. No pardon in the captain's face. But his eyes go
past the king, to the army: the wounded, the gun crews, the
standard-bearer. All waiting on one man.

                      CAPTAIN (CONT'D)
          You do not get to stop now.

Cael looks at them. No one can give this order for him. He
makes the hand close. He raises his eyes to the fleet.

                      CAEL
          Raise the banners.

The captain gives the signal. His eyes stay on the city.

The standard-bearer sees the signal. He tears the bindings
off. His banner opens, alone.

                      STANDARD-BEARER
                (in High Drakonic, subtitled)
          Burn the sky!
                      TRANSLATION
          Vael-bryna!

This time he is answered. The men nearest take it up, then
the line, ragged, banner after banner opening under the
ships. Not every man. Not the captain. Cael does not turn.

Above them, weapon housings open along the nearest hull.

The army under its banners. The ruined capital between. The
coalition filling the sky.

A great mechanical lock CLOSES.

SUPER: "CROWNFALL: KING OF KINGS"

One deep engine STROKE.

                   APPENDIX: NATIVE LINES

Native lines in story order. "Atlas": quoted from The Ash
and Aether Linguistic Atlas. "Built": made from its grammar,
and due a language check before recording. Capitals in the
say-it guides mark the stressed syllable, by the Atlas
rules.

Ihriq!
Scene 2. Kharzani, not subtitled. Ignite! Atlas: command
form of H-R-Q. Say: ih-RIQ, the q far back in the mouth,
with no w.

Iwqif. Lu=yaslam al-walad fi al-rash.
Scene 3. Kharzani, spoken as two lines. Stay. May the child
survive in the ash. Atlas: command of W-Q-F, then a line of
the lament "Six Seconds of Sea". Say: iw-QIF. lu-yas-LAM
al-wa-LAD fi al-RASH.

Brecath thone corune!
Scene 5. Old Aethel. Break the Crown! Built: plural command,
accusative object. Echoes "Se corun brec-de", the Crown
shattered. The Atlas writes each th with the single letter
thorn; it is spelled out here for the performers. Say:
BRE-cath THO-ne CO-ru-ne, th as in thin.

Va-ten-t el sveulea!
Scene 10. High Korvenic. Hold the stone. Built: ritual
command, va- plus stem plus -t. Say: va-TENT el sveu-LE-a,
the last t released on the beat.

Vael-bryna!
Scene 10. High Drakonic. The battle cry of Aethelgard. Atlas
gloss: ignite the upper air. Say: vael BRY-na. Vael is one
syllable. The letter "y" is a central vowel, not "ee".

No words, scenes 5 and 6: the navigator turns his face to a
thing a moment before it is struck. His four even strikes
are the Solforiz clapper code for a Seen report, a sight
four beats ahead.

Atlas terms used in Common speech: Cyning (king) and
Ash-Caliph.

Cut from this film and kept for the chronicles: Liora's
testimony, "Nasar al-malik=ayn. Damar al-malik=ayn." The
king saved; the king destroyed; each seen with my own eyes.
