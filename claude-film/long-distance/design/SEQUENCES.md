# Long Distance: the film, sequence by sequence

Times are a plan; a sequence may stretch or tighten by a few seconds when it is built, but **voiced starts are fixed** to the takes (a subtitle holds for its take + 0.35 s), and screenplay order is kept. Every line id appears exactly once. The look is B, Grain and Gloss, with C's staging (`design/DECISIONS.md`): his half and the empty half, the phone always in the empty half, one room seen from matching cameras by night and by day.

Acting notes in every sequence: he is gentle and ordinary, never a creep; small movements, heavy lids, a 2–3 px breath at 0.25 Hz. She is composed and tired, never cruel. Characters animate on twos (`FILM.twos`); camera, light, the phone's screen and its glow run on ones. Cameras are still unless a shot says otherwise; pushes are slow (0.2–0.5 % a second) and start, travel and land.
