## Fix list for cut 3

### Shared: likeness (the waterfall and app in `film/phone.js`, the strip in `film/sets/living.js`)
1. **One man, one woman, in every picture.** The man in the photo-booth strip reads lighter, with a beard; the man in the waterfall is much darker; the hero is neither. The strip, the waterfall, the lock screen and the app portrait must show the same two people as the rig's Him and Her: the same skin, hair and features, younger in the strip and the generated pictures.
2. **The generated images still read as illustrated avatars** (191, 389, 466). They should read as photographs that are subtly too perfect. Never caricature them.

### Shared: the rig (`film/rig.js`)

   - The bag: blob hands and a spare thumb (364).
   - The wardrobe: both arms end in cuffs with no hands (314).
   - Q04: the phone arm comes from the bottom of the frame, detached (124.5).
   - Q05: the bus hand reads as a foot.
   - The trade: four parallel tubes (427).
   - Q08: an arm like a pole puts the phone down (262); the door arm comes out of his chest (280).
4. **Her age.** At the door she reads younger than the photographs. "Older by exactly the time that has passed": give her tired eyes, faint lines and a slightly heavier face. Not old: a few years.
5. **Daylight faces.** They are still blotchy in the two-shots and her singles (288, 356, 403).
6. **Back collars** still read as neck pillows (88, 124.5).
7. **Sitting up and lying down** pivot like a plank (261.5, 485).
8. **Hand colour.** The hands in the inserts are peach-orange against his brown face (Q02, Q06).
