# Long Distance: review of the three looks (round 1)

Two independent reviewers saw only the nine frames and the brief: an art director and a cinematographer/story editor. Neither saw the code or the look documents. Both recommend **Look B, Grain and Gloss**.

## Why B
- It is the only look in which both of the film's hinges read at a glance on the board: the photo-booth strip rhyming with the waterfall picture, and the real woman set against her image.
- Its material carries the clue for a second watch. Grain is real, gloss is the phone.
- Her copper hair and freckles tie the strip, the generated image and the real woman together.

## Defects both reviewers found (sent back to each look for a fix round)

| Frame | Agreed defects |
| --- | --- |
| A1 | The phone hangs upright above the pillow on a stiff, overlong arm, so it reads as scrolling, not a hand on a sleeping shoulder. A smear covers his eye. An orange blot sits on the wall. |
| A2 | The fridge strip is a 40px sliver lost in the dark. His neck is a long tube. The pale rectangle reads as a TV. His eyeline goes to the pan, not the phone. |
| A3 | Her raised arm is a flat grey flap over her head. The phone hand has no forearm. Her face is a chalk mask. The bag has no zip. The phone image is unreadable. |
| B1 | The phone stands on its end like a lamp. He lies on his back facing the camera, not the pillow. His face is lit away from the phone. The pillows are flat slabs. |
| B2 | The phone is tablet-sized. The generated faces are crooked, not "too even". Nobody laughs in the strip. His hands are unreadable. A light wedge on the fridge has no source. |
| B3 | The phone is oversized and floats above her fingers. He looks at her, though the brief says he looks at neither. The bag arm is a stick. His body is a boulder. |
| C1 | Too dark at board size. The lamps read as speakers. The phone is lens-up but throws a sideways halo. His legs show through the duvet. |
| C2 | We see only his back, so the beat is lost. The pan is held out at arm's length and the arm is broken. The phone image is tiny and flat, the same style as the strip. |
| C3 | The screen faces the camera, not him. "Her:" is greyed almost out of sight. Her hand is at her jaw, not the back of her neck. His skin and build don't match C1 and C2. Slate text is burned into the bars (all C frames). |

## Consistency
- **A:** his hair changes in every frame.
- **B:** his face changes width and tone between frames. Otherwise B is the most consistent look.
- **C:** his skin tone and build jump in C3.

## What the reviewers suggest B borrows from C
C's matching room and half-and-half staging: the phone in the empty half, and the pale rectangle directly behind her in moment 3.
