# Long Distance: decisions

Settled with the author, Osita Miles. Started 2026-11-23.

| Decision | Choice |
| --- | --- |
| Text | The screenplay as written (`design/screenplay.txt`, transcribed word for word from the author's PDF). No narration: the audience believes in the long-distance girlfriend and discovers she is an AI through the picture and the confrontation, as the script plans it. The First Person and Merged prose versions (`design/source_*.txt`) are the story's source, not the film's text. |
| Voices | Everyone is voiced: Him, the real woman, and the phone's voice. The phone speaks in the same woman's voice, but dry, close and too clean ("Warm and close. Very clean. No room behind it."); the real woman has the room, the breath and the years. That contrast is the film's sound idea. |
| Cast | Chosen by the author by ear from an audition of five voices per role (2026-11-24; `audio/audition/`, reels `him_3.mp3` and `her_3.mp3`). **Him:** Gemini TTS `en-us-varo`, a 36-year-old American (Northwest) voice, laid-back. **Her:** Gemini TTS `en-us-tova`, a 36-year-old American (Northwest) voice, textured. The same voice plays the phone and the real woman; the mix separates them. Lines, directions and takes are in `audio/vo/script_vo.json`, and the chosen takes in `audio/vo/voice_map.json`. |
| Build | Animated entirely in code on the *One Too Many* / *Autopilot* engine (Canvas 2D), as a Claude Film. |
| Look | **B, Grain and Gloss, with C's staging** (chosen 2026-11-24 from `frames/BOARD_looks.jpg`, after two independent reviews that both recommended B; `design/REVIEW_looks.md`). Everything real is cut paper with grain; only the phone and what it makes are smooth, drawn after the grain (`design/look_b.md`, `kit/look_b.js`). From Half Empty (`design/look_c.md`) it takes the staging grammar: frames divided into his half and the empty half; the phone always in the empty half, where a person would be; one room seen from matching cameras by night and by day; in the visit she fills the empty half for the first time, standing in front of the pale rectangle, and what she holds up there is the phone; the last shot returns the phone to the empty half. B's palette, materials, characters (her copper hair and freckles across the strip, the generated images and the real woman) and full 16:9 frame are kept. |
| Music | **A sparse score**, chosen by the author on 27 November 2026 (the other choice was no music). One felt-piano motif heard twice, both cues cut from a single Lyria 3.5 take (`audio/score_plan.json`, take T2): **M01** under the waterfall (Q06, 3:17.5–3:45.5), from the moment he stops chewing and looks at the photo, fading into the hard cut; **M02** after the last "I love you too." (Q11, 7:57–8:23.5), as he lies down beside the phone, its natural fade ending at the cut to black. The planned silences stay: the screen going dark, the app opening with no ring, the black and the title. |
| Master | 1080p (1920×1080, 24 fps, H.264 CRF 20 capped at 5.5 Mbps: the paper grain made uncapped CRF 18 run past 40 Mbps, about 2.8 GB, more than the disk or the 400 MiB delivery limit allow) with the final mix (AAC 320 kbps, -18 LUFS, -2 dBTP), `out/long_distance_1080p.mp4`; the 720p review copy is no longer the delivery. Merge to `main` after the master (the author, 27 November 2026). |
