# Hall v22, critic C: direction and cut

File judged: `out/review/film_v22_hall.mp4` (48 s, 1151 frames). Picture and cut only.

## Verdict

No. As cut, this does not play as a scene from a Villeneuve film. Five of the hall's eight frames are composed and lit like the films named, but they do not add up to one room, one light, one woman, one man or one continuous event. The hall is below the council scene. The yard is still a trailer-paced montage across three different yards.

The three things that sink it:

1. **29.21–32.50** (Serath turns, "We were chosen."). A different-looking woman, lit like a portrait sitting, pulling faces.
2. **36.00 → 36.04**. The two people swap ends of the hall in one frame, day becomes dusk, and a wax seal blinks on and off a scroll for 4.9 s.
3. **The room is shown once**, for 2.9 s, in the dark, before the light comes in. The floor of orders, which is the point of the scene, is never seen lit.

## 2. Performance and motion

- **7.83–11.71** Serath close. Alive and restrained: the head comes up, one slow blink at 11.0. The best acting in the sequence.
- **14.33–20.71** Mouth and jaw move like speech. Good.
- **44.96–47.96** Eyes down, lids lift at 46.0–46.25. True. Then she stares into the lens for 1.7 s.
- **29.21–32.50** Over-acted. Narrowed side-eye at 29.83–30.25, puffed pout at 31.29, both eyes shut with pursed lips at 31.50, round "O" with raised brows at 31.71. An admiral in these films barely moves her mouth.
- **7.00 and 11.71** Karst is a mask. Frontal, symmetrical, hands clasped, evenly lit, eyes on the lens, almost no motion.
- **3.54–5.29** Two frozen starers. The woman looks straight down the lens at 5.21.
- **32.50–36.04** A good slow turn until 35.4. His eyes reach the lens at 35.50 and stay there.
- **Looks into the lens**: 3.75–5.29, 7.00–7.83, 11.71–12.54, 35.50–36.04, 46.5–47.96. Five shots of sixteen. The council uses direct address as a system; here it comes and goes, so it reads as actors noticing the camera.
- **Wrong motion**:
  - 1.08–1.17: Serath flings her arms out as if she stumbles on the ramp, and the cut lands on the blur.
  - 3.54: a jump cut inside a locked two-shot (profile at frame 84, turned at frame 85).
  - 6.4–7.0: the aide folds to his knees and puts his head down, weightless. It reads as a ritual bow, not a fall.
  - 23.75–26.25: heavy plank doors swing at constant speed with no weight.
  - 15.0–20.7: the carving grows under her finger.
  - 36.79–40.83: the seal and string morph.
- **Camera**: 13 of 16 shots are dead still from first frame to last. Only the lamp shot drifts, the shard pushes in about 17% and the last face creeps in about 3.5%. A run of still tableaux is a large part of why it reads "staged".

The yard averages 1.73 s a shot with three shots under one second. That is action-trailer pace. Villeneuve's arrivals (the herald on Caladan, the landing at Arrakeen) are wides held long enough to feel the ceremony and the scale.

The hall averages 4.2 s, no faster than the council (median 3.7 s, range 2.1–6.8 s). The time is in the wrong places. The council opens on its room for 5.8 s and every single after it has the same columns and cloud behind it. The hall gives its room 2.9 s and then never shows slab, floor and opening in one frame again.

The Reverend Mother's test sets the room and the seated figure before any close-up, holds each face through whole sentences, and gives the box and the needle their own seconds. Louise's first entry into the shell gives every threshold its own held shot: the lift, the shaft, the turn of gravity, the walk, the chamber, the screen. Here the threshold is skipped altogether (14.29 to 14.33) and the reveal is missing.

Cut points on action: 23.58 to 23.62 is good (his hands go to the doors in the wide and are on them in the closer shot). 1.21 lands mid-stumble. 7.00 lands mid-collapse.

**The single shot that would do most**: the hall wide after the shutters open. Camera low at the slab end, looking down the hall to the white doorway. A blade of daylight runs down the floor and falls off to black at the walls. Row after row of sheets under stones comes out of the dark, each stone throwing a shadow toward us. Serath, back to us and small, walks into them and stops at the first row. Karst, a silhouette, comes in from the opening toward a lit lamp set on the floor in the foreground. Hold 6–8 s with a slow push. It shows the floor lit, puts both people where Q10 needs them, plants the lamp and joins daylight to lamplight.

The one shot to replace is 29.21.

## 6. Faults ranked, with fixes

1. **29.21–32.50, the turn.** Wrong face, wrong hair, wrong light, mugging. It is the one hall frame with no counterpart in the study sheet and the one a viewer will call staged.
   - FREE: drop it. Lay "We were chosen." off-screen over the head of the next shot (32.50–33.4, while his back is still to us) so his turn becomes the answer to her voice.
   - NEW FOOTAGE: same beat. Near-profile at the dark slab, one cold edge of daylight on one side of the face and the other side black, a quarter turn toward the light and not toward camera, lips barely moving, hair as at 7.83.
2. **36.04–40.92, the lamp shot and the cut into it.** Teleport, day to dusk, lamp from nowhere, morphing seal, wrong sleeve, 4.9 s.
   - FREE: drop it and cut 35.42 straight to the shard. He looks, the thing, she looks. Three shots, no contradiction.
   - FREE, if it must stay: use 37.92–38.60 only (roll tied, sealed sheet in front, hand still), slowed to about 45%.
   - NEW FOOTAGE: one simple action. His hand lifts a stone off a flat sheet whose seal is there from the first frame; cream sleeve; lamp already burning; doorway behind blown to white with her as a pale figure in it.
3. **The room is never shown lit; no entry; no move.**
   - FREE: run the wide (20.71–23.62) long. First 2.3 s at 35–40%, last 0.6 s at full speed for his hands going to the doors, about 6.5 s in all. Nothing in it moves except him.
   - NEW FOOTAGE: the reveal wide in section 4.
4. **Yard pace and order.** Three shots under a second, a jump cut, a stumble, the speaker shown mute between his lines, the fall still on screen. FREE: the re-cut in section 7.
5. **14.33–20.71, the carving draws itself.**

   - FREE: grade the slab down about a stop on the left 60% of frame so it reads as the dark stone of the other shots. Her rim light is on the right and is untouched.
6. **Karst is not one man in one coat.**
   - FREE, partial: desaturate and lift the brown duster at 23.62–29.21 toward pale grey; pull the cream at 32.50 toward grey.

7. **Lens stares.** FREE: end Karst's turn at 35.42. Leave the last face by 46.7. Use Karst's doorway shot once only.
8. **44.96, face on a void.**
