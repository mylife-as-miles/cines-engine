A fresh reader with no part in the draft, 26 November. Read-only. It read the prompt, opened the six reference images at full size, the storyboard, the screenplay's scene 5, the shot list and song plan, the lessons, the earlier montage prompts and their results, and frames of the film as cut v16. Verdict: **send after the listed changes.** They are made in `prompts/sent/Q07_v1.txt`; the table of what changed is in `prompts/Q07.md`.

## Findings, most damaging first

1. **The wall's reference puts a water wheel and two people into shot 4.** ENV_E06's left third is a bucketed wheel many times a man's height with two figures at its foot; shot 4's own framing sentence described that composition. Attach a crop from the first open gate rightward (x 1420–3276).
2. **Shots 1 and 5 would come back as their references, each with a different wall.** ENV_E04's lower third shows a seven-arch viaduct with a village on the near side; ENV_E07 shows the wall as a viaduct of about fourteen arches down the valley's side, and is the one flat, evenly lit plate. The four plates show four different walls. Attach E04 as underside and band only (x 283–2993, y 0–820) and E07 as valley only (x 0–1480, y 330–1404), two thirds of a stop darker; delete the sentence about the references being drawn from the ground. Judgement, not measurement: no aerial has been asked of this model here.
3. **Shot 4: the bird is black on black, about 3 % of the frame, and untimed.** Move the camera close to the open gate so that it fills the left half of the frame and the bird is against the light in it; time the fall; give the last two seconds to the grass.
4. **Shot 2: four changes in 2.8 s, and the change of light has nothing to show on.** Say what the window shows before and after; keep her face readable; move the chin as well as the eyes; add "a grey studio backdrop" to the negatives; say she is alone and seen nowhere else.
5. **Shot 1 is a still by its own numbers.** At a kilometre on a 24 mm lens the cutter is four pixels. Give its size in the frame, place the cloud it leaves, lock the camera off, and attach one view of the cutter, not the four-view sheet. "Sinks", used five times of a boat, is a water word.
6. **No shot can fill a bar.** A bar is 2.857 s and the ends of every shot are trimmed; returned lengths drift. Recommended 16 s (about $3.53); a same-money alternative at 14 s gives the time to the cutter, the face and the bird. **The 14 s alternative was used: it is what the author was shown.**
7. **Scale and sky contradict each other between shots.** The mass hangs over the plain, not over Sarn Vale (the floating rock was taken out of that reference on purpose); the wall is about a hundred feet high, so "two hundred metres up" above it is wrong; "looking steeply down" contradicts a strip of horizon.
8. **Words that name things no frame should show:** "the great wall", "a kingdom of white marble", "full of people", "a cockpit window", "the stumps of a city", "ranks", "lanes and squares".

Not taken up: painting the lone figure out of ENV_E05 (an edit of an approved image).
