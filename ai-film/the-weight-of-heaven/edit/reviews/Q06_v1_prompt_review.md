# Q06 v1: independent review of the prompt before it is paid for

A fresh reader with no part in the draft, 26 November. Read-only: it read the prompt, the six reference images, the storyboard, the screenplay, Q04's and Q05's prompts and records, the lessons, and frames of the council scene as it stands in cut v15. It was not told what the author of the draft thought was weak. Verdict: **send after the listed changes.** All are made in `prompts/sent/Q06_v1.txt`; the table of what changed is in `prompts/Q06.md`.

## Findings, most damaging first

1. **Shot 3 repeated the open pause that cost Q05 two seconds, and the look was too small to read.** "He lets a moment pass" and "(after a still moment)" were both in Q05's shot of him, which came back with 2.9 s of silence and ran 5.3 s for 3.2 (lesson 96). A small turn with the eyes far to the side is a sideways glance: sly, not warm. The simile "as a father's does at a grown child" names two more people. Changed to a timed eighth of a turn and a plain description of his face.
2. **Both door shots look at a wall with no tone given, and the door was left to the model.** The one earlier shot toward a wall (Q04 shot 5) came back dark slate. The reference shows the left leaf swung back against the wall, the right leaf shut, and a bay of plain wall before the door; none of it was written. Changed in the lock and, in the same words, in both shots.
3. **Shot 6: her entrance crossed in front of Cassiel, and its times were faster than a walk.** One white coat over another at 480p in the busiest shot; about 2 m/s where the physics lock says a real walking pace. "Her eyes on the doorway" named eyes on a back. Changed: she is in the frame from its first frame, on its right half, and stays to the right of him.
4. **Shot 4 was overfull and ended on its weakest picture, and the lock said she keeps her place.** A bow, a turn, an exit and a half-second hold in 2.8 s; with the lens focused on her, nothing is sharp once she is gone. Changed: the bow timed, the hold dropped, the lock rewritten.
5. **The off-screen lines: the wording that kept a mouth shut in Q05 had been thinned.** Shot 1 had lost "a man's" and "where she is looking". Shot 2 puts twelve words over the only face in frame, a man's: "He breathes slowly" is a mouth instruction, "as his own name is spoken" can be read as him saying it, "a sentry" can bring a weapon. The reviewer's estimate, not a measurement: shot 1 unlikely to move; shot 2 perhaps one in three, on a face about 40 pixels high, with both lines replaceable in sound. Changed as proposed.
6. **The seal had no "before", another open "moment", and "crest".** Lesson 14: the film's first seal came back stamped before the press, with wax on the glove. Changed: the wax smooth and plain before, the press and lift timed, all the wax on the page after.
7. **The voice was asked to sound far away for 15 of its 19 words.** Untested, and a roomy voice cannot be dried. Changed to Q05's sentence: close and clear in every shot.
8. **As a film: "Cassiel waits for the seal" was not on the screen** (put to the author as a choice). His bow was cued only by her passing. Taken up in its lighter form: the last shot opens with his eyes on the table, where the order was sealed, before they come to her. Also taken up: a 22nd second for the last hold.

Named by the reviewer and not acted on: Serath's face while she hears the condition that costs her the command is missing; it would need a cut in mid-sentence, untested with this model.
