## What changed from cut v22, and on whose note

**The yard**
- **Out:** her stumble and the aide's fall (B, C; the author had asked for the tripping to go); the two watchers once they stare into the lens (A, B, F); the boat wide that ended mid-stumble; **Karst in the doorway, both times.** Every critic scored that shot lowest in the yard (a still, frontal figure on black, looking into the lens), and the author's stored note says a man in a doorway reads as cheap television. He is now a voice over Serath's two close shots and is first seen opening the shutters. *This goes further than any critic asked: B, C and F kept him once. If the author wants him seen in the yard, the take has him full-figure between two stone piers at natural speed, and it is a small change.*
- **In:** the cutter over the wall, Q08 round 2's own first shot, which no cut had used. It opens on the descent's last frame by design; here it is registered to that frame (0.4 % larger, 11 px across, 7 px down), matched in level and in grain (frame-to-frame difference 4.3 before the join, 4.4 to 4.7 after, measured in the file) and joined by a four-frame dissolve. It gives the arrival its one frame with size.
- Serath between the dark backs is shown almost whole (1.12×, was 1.35×: F, "cut at the shins") and from the frames before the aide's knee folds.
- The cut to her close shot now lands on the song's last hit (F: the hit came 3.5 frames after the cut).
- Her profile runs 12 frames longer, so Karst's second sentence has 0.57 s of air after it (F: it had 0.26 s).
- One grade over the yard, the first round's shots brought to the second round's colour (A, fault 9).
- All of it now runs at the takes' own speed except the dark-backs shot (57 %).

**The hall**
- The join from the yard: both profiles recropped toward each other (F's numbers) and ten frames taken off the head of the slab shot, where her mouth hung open before her voice. Still not an exact match (see below).
- The slab profile ends before the carved marks redraw themselves under her finger (C). Her reading runs on over the wide, which is held 6.4 s and cropped to lose the side door and the pale papers (B, C).
- The shutters shot is left while Karst is still speaking (B: he stood on his mark for 3.2 s), so "within forty years." is heard on Serath.
- Her turn is 0.7 s: eyes shut, the head begins to move, cut (A, B, C all scored the full turn lowest: studio light, a turn out to the camera, a different-looking woman). It is mirrored, so she stays on the side of the frame she was on, facing the slab on the left (B: the slab jumped sides; F: wrong side of the line). The carved marks are reversed for those 0.7 s; they are not letters of any alphabet.
- "We were chosen." is heard over Karst's back, and his turn answers it (B, C). His shot ends before his eyes reach the lens.

## Not fixed, because an edit cannot fix it

- **The five lines the hall still lacks:** "You were sheltered." / "By then you had gardens." / "I drew where each one would fall." / "You sealed them." / "The marriage bell." Without them the lamp, the shard and her face say nothing about what the papers are.
- **Karst and Serath never share a frame in the yard**, and the yard has no wide of its own ground that holds them both (F, fault 2).
- **The hall's turn**, lit from the wrong side in the take; here it is only cut short. The critics' design for it: near profile at the dark slab, one cold edge of daylight, a quarter turn toward the light, never toward the lens.
- **The light after the doors open** (F, fault 1): no lamp is seen in the wide, Karst goes from the doorway to a lamp deep in the hall in one cut, and her last face is lit from above. The grade brings the levels together; it does not move a light.
- **The join from the yard** is closer but not registered: the head is still tipped differently on the two sides. An exact match needs the hall's first frame made from the yard's last.
- **Things that change between takes:** her gloves (white in the yard, grey at the slab), Karst's coat, the plain (basalt columns in one shot, rippled in the next), the slab's stone.
- 273 of the 889 new frames are slowed by interpolation (the dark-backs shot, the first part of the wide, her turn, the lamp, her last face); the first build had 332 of 905.
- The carved marks still read as Latin capitals in the slab profile.
- "Stone" may be heard as "stoned" in both voices (F, from a small speech model); the two larger models here hear "stone".
