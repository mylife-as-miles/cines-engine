## SCENE INTENT
This follows the sequence before directly. A white naval cutter from the kingdom that floats above the cloud has crossed the black plain. It passes along the top of the great black wall and sets down in the yard of an old stone fort. Serath, an officer of that kingdom, has never stood on the ground. She steps onto it and for the first time in her life feels her own weight: her knees give for a moment. Behind her, her aide falls outright. The people of the ground watch the sky people learn to stand. Nobody laughs. Then, close: the gold collar of her rank is heavy on her neck and pulls her head down, and she lifts her head against it.
