## SCENE INTENT
A small white naval cutter comes down beneath Orison, the kingdom that floats above the cloud, for the first time. Two separate views from the air, cut hard. In the first the cutter comes down out of the cloud under the kingdom's black underside, tiny in a band of daylight. In the second it is seen from high above, a white fleck crossing a vast black plain toward a far wall. Both are held, slow and enormous.
