Large-format digital (ARRI Alexa 65), spherical lenses, deep focus except in the close inserts, clean highlights. The camera floats: slow, smooth, slightly high.

SHOTS (each shot cuts exactly at its end time; nobody speaks):
1 (0–2.9 s) Opens exactly on @Image1, the same framing: the gallery opposite, the guests in white behind the balustrade, the banners below. The camera keeps gliding slowly to the left, about 1 m. Everyone stands still, gloved hands folded, eyes on the tower.

3 (5.7–8.6 s) Medium shot from the tower side, 40 mm, eye height, locked off. At 0.3 s the girl trips; the basket leaves her hands and drifts up and away through the air, slowly, petals floating out of it. At 1.8 s Serath, without turning her head, raises her right gloved hand and takes the basket out of the air; by 2.6 s she has handed it back to the girl. Serath's eyes stay on the tower.
4 (8.6–11.4 s) Close-up of her hands, 85 mm, shallow focus, locked off: the aide holds out the warrant, a pool of soft red wax at its foot. Serath's right hand in its white glove, the seal ring over the glove, presses the ring down into the wax at 0.8 s and lifts it by 2 s, leaving the red seal, an anchor in a ring of seven stars. Her eyes never go to the page.
5 (11.4–17.1 s) A held wide from the nave floor, 20 mm, low, locked off, looking up the aisle to the open tower; the guests in white stand small on both sides. Above the tower's open top hangs the barge, its four chains running taut down to the bell just above the beam. At 1.5 s the barge lets go, like a ship's crane setting a load down on a quay: the four chains run slack and fall in long, slow loops, and the bell drops a hand's breadth onto the stone beam and rests there, swaying a little, still by 4.5 s.
6 (17.1–20 s) Side on, level with the beam, 35 mm, wide enough to show the whole bell and the whole hammer together, locked off. The bell, six metres tall, hangs from the beam; beside it hangs the hammer on its long white timber arm. Its dark iron head, wrapped in pale leather on the striking face, is the size of a door: longer than a man is tall, half the height of the bell. From 0.3 s ropes draw the hammer slowly back, away from the bell, until it hangs high at the top of its swing at 2.3 s.
7 (20–22.9 s) From the aisle looking up at the tower, 28 mm, locked off: the hammer swings in and its iron head strikes the bell's side at 0.8 s; the bell shudders and sways a hand's breadth; the petals in the air tremble. The camera holds on the bell to the end of the shot.
