## THE WEIGHT OF HEAVEN — Q07 · BENEATH ORISON · THE UNDERSIDE

Reference mode · 14 s · 5 shots · 2.35:1 · photoreal live action · no speech · no music

## SCENE INTENT
Serath has lived her whole life on top of Orison, a kingdom of white marble that floats in the cloud. Now she comes down beneath it for the first time, in a small white naval cutter. Five separate views, cut hard, one clear thing in each: the cutter falls out of the cloud under the kingdom's underside; her face as she sees it; the black plain below; the ancient wall that carries the kingdom's weight, and what that weight does to a bird; the green valley beyond the wall, full of people. From below the kingdom is not beautiful. It is only enormous.

## GLOBAL LOCKS
Character identity — SERATH exactly as @Image 6: a woman of fifty-five, upright, short black hair going grey, a long white coat, a heavy gold collar at her throat, white gloves. From the sheet take face, hair and clothes only.
Wardrobe override — none: exactly as her sheet.
Location / environment continuity — One country, seen from the air and from the ground. Over everything hangs the underside of Orison exactly as @Image 1: a ceiling of raw black rock from one horizon to the other, roots hanging from it, iron chains as thick as towers hanging plumb from it, its upper edges lost in grey cloud. Under it lies the basalt plain exactly as @Image 2: black six-sided columns, broken and stepped, in ranks to the horizon. At the far edge of the plain, across the head of a green valley, the great wall exactly as @Image 3: one straight wall of fused black stone a mile long, smooth, without a seam or a tool-mark, with nine tall arched gates in a row; the two gates at one end are shut with black stone and seven are open, with dry ground and green valley seen through them. Beyond the wall, Sarn Vale exactly as @Image 4: green terraces, orchards, grey stone farms and walls, a road, the smoke of many hearths. @Image 1 to @Image 4 were drawn from the ground: shots 1, 3 and 5 see the same places from the air, at the height each shot gives.
Props and temporal state — The cutter exactly as @Image 5: a small white wooden boat that flies, clinker-built, ten metres long, with a little white pilothouse with pointed windows and a short spire, and two dark wooden runners under its keel. It hangs in the air level and quiet, keel down, and sinks at a steady pace.

## MOVIE COLOR
One colour for this whole country: cold blue-grey slate. Black rock, black basalt, grey cloud. The only bright thing is the pale band of day between the black ceiling and the black plain; the underside sits two stops under that band, its rock and chains still readable. The green of the valley is muted and wet under cloud. The white cutter and the gold at Serath's throat are the only clean things. Every shot is exposed for the band of day; blacks deep but never crushed; mist greys everything far off.
Influences, each with its purpose — Denis Villeneuve: Arrival, a small craft under the edge of a vast dark shape that enters from the top of the frame, mist between them; Dune, a face in profile at a cockpit window with the glass bright beside it, and small things on a vast plain. James Cameron: a look answered by what it looks at, she looks up and then down; size shown by a small known thing beside it; a cause and its effect in one frame, the open gate and the bird.
Camera package — ARRI Alexa 65, large format, spherical primes, fine grain. Shots 1, 3 and 5 are from the air on a steady camera that moves slowly. Shot 2 is inside the cutter, close, shallow focus. Shot 4 is low and locked off.

## VOICE DNA (immutable)
No one speaks.

## AUDIO DESIGN
High cold wind. The creak of the cutter's timber. From far above, the very low hum of the great mass, more felt than heard. In shot 4, grass hissing flat, wingbeats, one soft thud. In shot 5, a far dog and the valley's quiet. Natural sound only.

None. Shot 1 opens on a hard cut.

## PERFORMANCE ARC
SERATH: composed, in cloud → the shade of what she has lived on falls across her → she looks up at it → she looks down.

## THEMATIC / SYMBOLIC / STRUCTURAL LOGIC
Her kingdom is a ceiling over someone else's country. Its weight cannot be seen; it shows only in what it does: grass pressed flat, a bird that falls. The valley's smoke is the people who live under it.

## PHYSICS LOCKS
Normal speed and real weight throughout. The mass hangs perfectly still; its chains hang plumb. The cutter sinks level, at a steady pace. Cloud and mist drift slowly. The bird falls straight down at the speed of a dropped stone. Smoke rises and leans gently.
