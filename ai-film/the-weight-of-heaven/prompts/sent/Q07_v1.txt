# AUTEUR VIDEO PROMPT
## THE WEIGHT OF HEAVEN — Q07 · BENEATH ORISON · THE UNDERSIDE
References: @Image 1 — environment: the underside of the kingdom and the pale band of day under it · @Image 2 — environment: the basalt plain · @Image 3 — environment: the black wall and its gates · @Image 4 — environment: Sarn Vale, the valley beyond the wall · @Image 5 — the naval cutter · @Image 6 — character sheet: SERATH
Reference mode · 16 s · 5 shots · 2.35:1 · photoreal live action · no speech · no music

## SCENE INTENT
Serath has lived her whole life on top of Orison, the kingdom that floats above the cloud. Now she comes down beneath it for the first time, in a small white naval cutter. Five separate views, cut hard, one clear thing in each: the cutter comes down out of the cloud under the kingdom's underside; her face as she sees it; the black plain below; the ancient black wall at the plain's edge, and a bird that falls in front of its open gate; the green valley beyond the wall, its chimneys smoking. From below the kingdom is only enormous.

## GLOBAL LOCKS
Character identity — SERATH exactly as @Image 6: a woman of fifty-five, upright, short black hair going grey, a long white coat, a heavy gold collar at her throat, white gloves. From the sheet take face, hair and clothes only. Serath is seen in shot 2 and nowhere else.
Wardrobe override — none: exactly as her sheet.
Location / environment continuity — One country, seen from the air and from the ground. Over the whole basalt plain hangs the underside of Orison exactly as @Image 1: a ceiling of raw black rock, roots hanging from it, iron chains hanging plumb from it, each link longer than the cutter, its edges lost in grey cloud. Under it lies the basalt plain exactly as @Image 2: black six-sided columns, broken and stepped, in rows to the horizon. Along the far edge of the plain stands the black wall exactly as @Image 3: one straight wall of fused black stone a mile long and thirty metres high, smooth, with nine tall arched gates in a row, most of them open, the others shut with black stone; the plain is on the near side of it, and through each open gate the green valley is seen. Beyond the wall and lower than the plain, under grey cloud, Sarn Vale exactly as @Image 4: green terraces, orchards, grey stone farms and field walls, a road, the smoke of many hearths.
Props and temporal state — The cutter exactly as @Image 5: a small white wooden boat that flies, clinker-built, ten metres long, with a little white pilothouse with pointed windows and a short spire, a brass rail round its deck, and two dark runners under its keel. There is one cutter, alone in the sky. It hangs in the air level and quiet, keel down, and comes straight down at a slow steady pace.

## MOVIE COLOR
One colour for this whole country: cold blue-grey slate. Black rock, black basalt, grey cloud. The only bright thing is the pale band of day between the black ceiling and the black plain; the underside sits two stops under that band, its rock and chains still readable. The green of the valley is muted and wet under cloud. The white cutter and the gold at Serath's throat are the only clean things. Every shot is exposed for the band of day; blacks deep but never crushed; mist greys everything far off.
Influences, each with its purpose — Denis Villeneuve: Arrival, a small white boat under a vast dark ceiling, mist between them; Dune, a face in profile at a window, the glass beside it. James Cameron: a look answered by what it looks at; size shown by a small known thing beside it; a cause and its effect in one frame, the open gate and the bird.
Camera package — ARRI Alexa 65, large format, spherical primes, fine grain. Shots 1 and 5 are from the air and locked off. Shot 3 is from the moving cutter. Shot 2 is inside the cutter, close, shallow focus. Shot 4 is low and locked off.

## VOICE DNA (immutable)
No one speaks.

## AUDIO DESIGN
High cold wind. The creak of the cutter's timber. From far above, the very low hum of the great mass, more felt than heard. In shot 4, grass hissing flat, wingbeats, one soft thud. In shot 5, a far dog and the valley's quiet. Natural sound only.

## FIRST-FRAME ANCHOR
None. Shot 1 opens on a hard cut.

## SHOT-BY-SHOT BREAKDOWN
### SHOT 1
[TIME] 0.0–3.2 s
[CAM] extreme wide, 24 mm, from the air, level with the cutter and far from it; locked off. The only ground is the plain far below.
[BLOCK] The top two thirds of the frame are the underside of Orison as @Image 1: black rock, roots and chains, running out of the top and both sides of the frame. The bottom third is the pale band of day, with a thin strip of the black plain, greyed by mist, along the very bottom. Right of centre a bank of grey cloud hangs below the rock into the top of the pale band. Beside it one chain hangs from the rock down through the band, each of its links longer than the cutter. The cutter is small, one twenty-fifth of the frame wide, but plainly a white boat with a spire; at the first frame only its keel and runners show under the cloud.
[ACT] The cutter comes straight down out of the bottom of the cloud, level, a thin trail of vapour pulling off its spire. By 1.2 s all of it is clear, white against the pale band, clear air under its keel. By the last frame it is twice its own length lower, level with one link of the chain. Cloud drifts slowly across the rock. Nothing else moves.
[DIALOGUE] / V.O. none
[SFX] wind; the low hum of the mass.
[STATE OUT] The cutter small and white in the band of day, still coming down.

### SHOT 2
[TIME] 3.2–6.8 s
[CAM] close-up, 75 mm, inside the cutter's small pilothouse, at her eye height, shallow focus; the camera holds still.
[BLOCK] Serath alone in the frame, in profile, facing the left of the frame, from the collar up, her head on the right third, her hands below the frame. The left half of the frame is one tall pointed window, close beyond her face, soft. Behind her head, on the right, the white-painted boards of the pilothouse wall, soft. The only light comes through the window, onto her brow, nose, mouth and the gold at her throat.
[ACT] Her mouth is closed from the first frame to the last. At the first frame the whole window is bright grey-white cloud streaming past, its light bright and soft on her face; her eyes are level, on the glass. By 4.0 s the cloud tears away: the upper two thirds of the window go dark, the black rock of the underside, far off and soft, and a strip of pale day is left along the bottom of the glass. The light on her face falls by half and turns cold blue-grey; her eye and the line of her nose and mouth stay readable. By 4.6 s her chin has lifted a little and her eyes have gone up to the dark in the top of the window; they rest there, and one breath goes in through her nose. At 5.6 s her chin comes level, her eyes go down through the bottom of the glass, and they stay down to the cut.

[SFX] wind on the glass; the wood creaks.
[STATE OUT] Serath in profile in the cold shade, her chin level, her eyes down, her mouth closed.

[TIME] 9.2–12.8 s
[CAM] wide, 40 mm, low, at the height of the grass, close in front of the nearest open gate and a little to its right, looking through it and along the foot of the wall; locked off. Above the wall the sky is the dark lid of @Image 3, with a pale strip at the horizon on the right.

## PERFORMANCE ARC
SERATH: composed, in cloud → the shade of what she has lived on falls across her → she lifts her chin and looks up at it → she looks down.

## THEMATIC / SYMBOLIC / STRUCTURAL LOGIC
Her kingdom is a ceiling over someone else's country. Its weight cannot be seen; it shows only in what it does: grass pressed flat, a bird that falls. The valley's smoke is the people who live beyond the wall.

## PHYSICS LOCKS
Normal speed and real weight throughout. The mass hangs perfectly still; its chains hang plumb. The cutter comes down level, at a slow steady pace. Cloud and mist drift slowly. The bird falls straight down as dead weight, in free fall. Smoke rises and leans gently.
