## SCENE INTENT
This follows the sequence before directly. In an old dark hall on the plain, Karst, an engineer who went over to the people below long ago, has opened the shutters and shown Serath, Grand Admiral of the kingdom above, the plain outside. She has answered him with what she was taught. Now he answers her, without heat. Then he shows her the floor. It is covered with papers, hundreds of them: every sheet is an order of her kingdom to lift something heavy into the sky, every sheet carries her own mark in red wax, and on every sheet lies a stone from a house that was crushed when that weight came down somewhere else. He made the maps on them; she put her mark to them without looking. She walks the row and sees her mark again and again. At the end of the row lies the newest: a grey shard, three days old. She knows what it is. Every voice stays low. Nobody hurries.

## MOVIE COLOR
Black rock, pale paper, white cloth, and two lights. The daylight at the opening is cool, soft and grey. The lamp is warm: a small amber pool an arm's length across, which lights paper brightly, cloth softly and black rock hardly at all. Shot 1 is exposed for the sky in the opening, exactly as @Image 1: Karst is a dark shape against it with a line of light along his cheek. Shots 2 to 5 are low key, exposed for the lamplight on paper: outside the lamp's pool the hall is near black; a face lit by the lamp from below sits a stop under the paper and is never the brightest thing in the frame; far behind, the open shutters are a small pale shape, out of focus. The red of the wax and the gold of Serath's collar are the only strong colours.
Influences, with their purpose — Denis Villeneuve, Dune and Blade Runner 2049: a face in the dark held by a single small warm light; a small pale shape of daylight far behind a figure; the insert of a single object and nothing else; a hard thing heard on the face of the person it is said to. James Cameron: every look answered by what it looks at; the thing that explains is seen before the words that name it: the papers and the stones first, then what he says of them.
Camera package — ARRI Alexa 65, large format, spherical primes, fine grain. Shallow focus on faces. Every shot is on a steady camera and moves slowly or not at all.

## VOICE DNA (immutable)
SERATH: The high speech of Orison: a soft, unplaced Received Pronunciation, older and gentler than a modern one, with no regional mark. A woman of fifty-five with a low alto, warm but closed, a little grain at the bottom of the range. She speaks at a measured pace; she never hurries, and her voice stays low. Here her breath can be heard, a little heavier; her voice itself does not change.
KARST: The grammar of the court spoken with the vowels of the ground: an educated Received Pronunciation flattened toward a northern English plainness by nineteen years below. A man of fifty-six with a dry, tired baritone, a slight rasp, low energy. He is unhurried. His sentences end flat and falling. He is never angry. When something matters most he gets quieter, not louder.
No other person is in the hall.

## AUDIO DESIGN

## PERFORMANCE ARC
KARST: answers her without heat → gets quieter where it matters → shows her the floor and says only what it is.
SERATH: walks the row as an officer inspects → sees her own mark on every sheet → hears whose work it is and stops → at the last sheet knows the day and the place, and says its name.

## THEMATIC / SYMBOLIC / STRUCTURAL LOGIC
She is the cause, and she can count it. Paper takes the light and black rock does not. The newest stone is three days old.
