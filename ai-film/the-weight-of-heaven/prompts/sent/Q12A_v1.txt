## SCENE INTENT
Serath, Grand Admiral of a kingdom of cities held up in the sky, and Karst, an engineer of her own people who went over to the people of the ground long ago, are in an old hall that stands on a great flat of grey stone. She has just admitted that she sealed orders she never read. Now he has turned away from her to the tall opening in the end wall and looks out at the stone, and he tells her what it could do for her kingdom. She asks what it will cost. It plays as one quiet, unbroken moment: a man looking out, a woman watching his back, both voices low, nothing hurried.

## MOVIE COLOR
Dark stone, pale cloth, white sky; the gold of Serath's collar is the only strong colour. Both shots are low key: inside the hall almost everything is dark, and the opening is the brightest thing in the film at this moment, soft and white, never burnt out.
Influences, each with its purpose — Denis Villeneuve, Arrival and Blade Runner 2049: a dark back before a bright opening, with the thing looked at beyond it; stillness; a face close and dim against the dark. James Cameron: every look is answered by what it looks at.
Camera package — ARRI Alexa 65, large format, spherical primes, fine grain. Each shot is on a steady, planted camera and does not move.

## VOICE DNA (immutable)
KARST: The grammar of the court spoken with the vowels of the ground: an educated Received Pronunciation flattened toward a northern English bluntness by nineteen years below. A man of fifty-six with a dry, tired baritone, a slight rasp, low energy. He is unhurried. His sentences end flat. He is never angry. When something matters most he gets quieter, not louder.
SERATH: The high speech of Orison: a soft, unplaced Received Pronunciation, older and gentler than a modern one, with no regional mark. A woman of fifty-five with a low alto, warm but closed, a little grain at the bottom of the range. She speaks at a measured pace; she never hurries, and her voice stays low but fully voiced, never a whisper.
Only these two are in the hall.

## AUDIO DESIGN
The sound of a bare stone room, recorded as it is: close, dry and very quiet, with a little of the room on the voices. Through the opening, far off and faint, air moving over open ground. Both voices are close and clear. Nothing else sounds.
