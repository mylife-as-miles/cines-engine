## SCENE INTENT
The yard of an old stone fort on the ground, full of refugees grey with rock dust. Serath, an officer of the kingdom in the sky, has just landed and is walking through them; she is never seen in these two shots. First: Marl, who lost her valley two days ago, is handing out spears. She puts one into the hand of a farmer who is alone. His free hand goes to the child's wooden bowl at his belt and stays there. Then both of them watch Serath pass. Second: at the fort's door waits Karst, once an engineer of that kingdom, now the man who speaks for the ground. He tells her, without heat, what the ground has done.

When something matters most he gets quieter, not louder.
