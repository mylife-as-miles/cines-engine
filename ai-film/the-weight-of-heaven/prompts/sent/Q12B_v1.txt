## SCENE INTENT
Serath, Grand Admiral of a kingdom of cities held up in the sky, and Karst, an engineer of her own people who went over to the people of the ground long ago, are in an old hall that stands on a great flat of grey stone. He has told her the stone could bear her whole kingdom, and she has asked his price. He names it. She says what she takes it to mean. He corrects her with the gentlest thing he says in the film. Then neither speaks, and we see the two of them and the stone in one frame. It plays as one quiet, unbroken conversation: every voice low, nothing hurried.

## GLOBAL LOCKS
Character identity — KARST exactly as @Image 3 and as he is in @Image 5: a man of fifty-six, grey in his dark hair and short beard, a long pale stained engineer's coat, the same coat in every shot. SERATH exactly as @Image 2 and as she is in @Image 4: a woman of fifty-five, short tightly curled black hair going grey, a long white coat, a heavy gold collar at her throat. From the sheets take face, hair and clothes only. Each face, costume, voice and line stays with its one person.
Wardrobe override — none: both exactly as their sheets. No hands are in any frame.
Location / environment continuity — an old hall of dark masonry. In its end wall is one tall opening, its wooden leaves folded back, as @Image 1. Outside lies a flat of dark grey stone, empty to a level horizon, under a white overcast sky. No sun, no people, no buildings out there.
Light — one source, the same in every shot: the white overcast daylight that comes in through the tall opening. Karst stands near the opening with his back to it: the light is behind him, on his hair, his ear and his shoulder, and his face is in soft shade, as @Image 5. Serath is a few steps inside the hall, facing the opening: the daylight falls on the front of her face from the left of the frame and the back of her head is in the dark, as @Image 4. A small oil lamp stands on the floor below the frame; it adds only a faint warm touch from low down and is never seen.
Where each is — Karst stands near the opening and has turned back to face her. Serath is a few steps inside the hall, low beside a rock on the floor; she looks up at him. Karst is on the left and looks toward the right of the frame; Serath is on the right and looks toward the left of the frame. In shots 1, 2 and 3 each is framed alone.
Props and temporal state — nothing is carried and nothing is handled.

## MOVIE COLOR
Dark stone, pale cloth, white sky; the gold of Serath's collar is the only strong colour. Every shot is low key, as @Image 4 and @Image 5: the faces dim, the room dark around them. In shot 4 the opening is the brightest thing in the frame, soft and white, never burnt out.
Influences, each with its purpose — Denis Villeneuve, Arrival and Dune: a face close and dim against the dark; in the last shot a dark shoulder near the lens and, beyond it, a figure standing against the light; stillness. James Cameron: every look is answered by what it looks at.
Camera package — ARRI Alexa 65, large format, spherical primes, fine grain. Every shot is on a steady, planted camera and does not move.

## VOICE DNA (immutable)
KARST: The grammar of the court spoken with the vowels of the ground: an educated Received Pronunciation flattened toward a northern English bluntness by nineteen years below. A man of fifty-six with a dry, tired baritone, a slight rasp, low energy. He is unhurried. His sentences end flat. He is never angry. When something matters most he gets quieter, not louder. His voice is the same voice in shot 1 and in shot 3.
SERATH: The high speech of Orison: a soft, unplaced Received Pronunciation, older and gentler than a modern one, with no regional mark. A woman of fifty-five with a low alto, warm but closed, a little grain at the bottom of the range. She speaks at a measured pace; she never hurries, and her voice stays low but fully voiced, never a whisper.
Only these two are in the hall.

## AUDIO DESIGN
The sound of a bare stone room, recorded as it is: close, dry and very quiet, with a little of the room on the voices. Through the opening, far off and faint, air moving over open ground. Both voices are close and clear. In shot 4 only the room and the far air. Nothing else sounds.
