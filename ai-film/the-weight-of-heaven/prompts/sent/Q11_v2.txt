## SCENE INTENT
Serath, Grand Admiral of a kingdom of the sky, and Karst, an engineer of her own people who went over to the people below long ago, are in an old hall on the plain. On the floor between them lies a broken piece of grey rock from the valley where her wedding was held, and she has just recognised it. He tells her the figures of what was set down there. She has laid her bare hand on the rock, below the frame, and she tells him the truth about the orders she marked. He answers with the truth about himself. It plays as one unbroken, quiet conversation: two faces, each framed alone, every voice low, nothing hurried.

## GLOBAL LOCKS
Character identity — SERATH exactly as @Image 2 and as she is in @Image 4: a woman of fifty-five, short tightly curled black hair going grey, a long white coat, a heavy gold collar at her throat. KARST exactly as @Image 3: a man of fifty-six, grey in his dark hair and short beard, a long pale stained engineer's coat, the same coat in both his shots. From the sheets take face, hair and clothes only. Each face, costume, voice and line stays with its one person.
Wardrobe override — none: both exactly as their sheets. No hands are in any frame.
Location / environment continuity — an old hall of dark masonry, as @Image 1: behind each face only its dark stone wall, out of focus.
Light — the same in every shot, and as @Image 4: soft, cool, overcast daylight from a tall opening off-screen, falling on each face from the side that face looks toward; the other side of the head is in the dark. A small oil lamp stands on the floor below the frame; it adds only a faint warm touch from low down and is never seen.
Where each is — they are an arm's length apart. Serath is low, beside the rock on the floor; Karst stands. Each is framed alone, from the chest up. Serath is on the right and looks toward the left of the frame; Karst is on the left and looks toward the right of the frame. What they look at, the rock and her hand on it, is on the floor between them, below every frame.
Props and temporal state — nothing is carried and nothing is handled.

## MOVIE COLOR
Dark stone, white cloth, grey daylight; the gold of Serath's collar is the only strong colour. All four shots are low key, as @Image 4: one side of the face in soft cool light, the rest falling off into the dark, the background near black.
Influences, each with its purpose — Denis Villeneuve, Arrival and Blade Runner 2049: a face close and dim against the dark; stillness; a line said only after the thing has been looked at. James Cameron: every look is answered by what it looks at.
Camera package — ARRI Alexa 65, large format, spherical primes, fine grain. Shallow focus on the eyes. Every shot is on a steady, planted camera and does not move.

## VOICE DNA (immutable)
SERATH: The high speech of Orison: a soft, unplaced Received Pronunciation, older and gentler than a modern one, with no regional mark. A woman of fifty-five with a low alto, warm but closed, a little grain at the bottom of the range. She speaks at a measured pace; she never hurries, and her voice stays low but fully voiced, never a whisper.
KARST: The grammar of the court spoken with the vowels of the ground: an educated Received Pronunciation flattened toward a northern English plainness by nineteen years below. A man of fifty-six with a dry, tired baritone, a slight rasp, low energy. He is unhurried. His sentences end flat. He is never angry. When something matters most he gets quieter, not louder. His voice is the same voice in shot 1 and in shot 3.
Only these two are in the hall.

## AUDIO DESIGN
A bare hall of rock, close and dry, with a little of its own echo on the voices. Low, far off, the open air of a plain. Both voices are close and clear. No drone, no boom, no music.
