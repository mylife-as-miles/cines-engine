## SCENE INTENT
Orison, a white kingdom that floats in the cloud, has been losing height for three days. Cloud that used to lie far below now stands level with the floor of its council chamber, and a cup of wine on the council table will not stop trembling. An old engineer tells the Council how far the city has come down and what holding its height would cost: a valley. Nobody in the room is troubled except the man who has to say it, and the admiral at the arch, who hears the valley's name.

## MOVIE COLOR
White stone in cloud, a little cooler and greyer than a wedding day: pale grey marble, grey-white cloud, white cloth. The gold of the cup and of Serath's collar and the dark red of the wine are the only colour. Every shot is exposed for the cloud in the arches: the room sits just under it, each face is lit softly from the side of the arches and falls off on the other side, and a face is never the brightest thing in the frame. Whites keep their detail. No cast shadows.
Influences, each with its purpose — Denis Villeneuve: Dune and Arrival, small people held in a large symmetrical room; a man who speaks with his back to us; frontal faces, centred, in shallow focus; the quiet before a hard word. James Cameron: every cause seen before it is explained, and each person's place in the room shown before they speak.
Camera package — ARRI Alexa 65, large format, spherical primes, fine grain. Deep focus on the wide shots, shallow focus on the cup and on faces. The camera floats: every shot drifts slowly and smoothly, as if weightless, and never shakes.

## VOICE DNA (immutable)
THE ENGINEER OF ORISON: The high speech, older and technical, a little out of practice at court. A man of seventy-two with a thin, reedy tenor. He is hesitant: he starts sentences and stops, clears his throat, and is exact with numbers and vague with consequences. He is apologetic and would rather not say it.
THE HIEROPHANT: The high speech at its purest: a liturgical Received Pronunciation, old-fashioned and round. A man of seventy-four with a rich, resonant bass-baritone, deep and full in the breath, a voice used to filling vast rooms without effort. Every sentence has a liturgical cadence and comes to rest on a falling end, like a pronouncement. He is perfectly steady and has never been in doubt.
No one else speaks.

## AUDIO DESIGN
A quiet stone room open to the sky: soft moving air and nothing else under the voices. In shots 1 and 2 only, far outside, three or four bells in different towers clank faintly, out of time with each other; they thin away before anyone speaks. Very close in shot 1: the fine dry buzz of the cup's gold foot shivering on the marble. Voices are close and clear, with a short airy tail. No music.

## THEMATIC / SYMBOLIC / STRUCTURAL LOGIC

## PHYSICS LOCKS
