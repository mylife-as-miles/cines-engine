## THE WEIGHT OF HEAVEN — Q03B · ANVIL VALE · THE MOUNTAIN LETS GO

Reference mode · 12 s · 3 shots · 2.35:1 · photoreal live action

## SCENE INTENT
The cost lands below. In the kingdom overhead a great bell has just been struck, and its weight has gone into the ground. In silence the face of the mountain lets go. The farmer has his daughter under his arm before the sound arrives, and the whole valley runs. Silence first, then weight, then fear.

## GLOBAL LOCKS
Character identity — The FARMER exactly as @Image 1: forties, weathered, short dark beard, his back bent by a life of carrying; he stoops even when he runs. His DAUGHTER exactly as @Image 2: six, small, one fair braid, grave grey eyes.
Wardrobe override — none: patched grey-brown wool exactly as the sheets, damp, wet earth on his hands and boots.
Location / environment continuity — Anvil Vale exactly as @Image 3, and lit exactly as @Image 3: black ploughed field; a low drystone wall; a low buttressed stone house with a mud path from its door; the flat-topped mountain, the Anvil, across the field; overhead the dark rock underside of a floating kingdom, a stone ceiling over the whole valley, with one pale band of cold daylight under it along the horizon. Overcast dusk, no sun, wet ground, cold wind, mist.
Props and temporal state — This follows the previous sequence without a break: dust is already sifting from the Anvil's rim; the farmer stands in the field with his hoe; the bowl, exactly as @Image 6 and full of water, stands on the wall in front of his daughter.

## MOVIE COLOR
One colour for this whole world: cold blue-grey slate. Black wet earth, grey stone, grey-brown wool. The only bright thing is the pale band under the dark mass; the only warmth is skin and the wood of the bowl. Every shot is exposed for that band, everything else a stop under, blacks deep but never crushed. People are dark shapes against it, lit cold from one side. The dust is pale grey-brown and soft, and mist greys everything far off.
Influences, each with its purpose — Denis Villeneuve: Arrival, a small human before a vast shape, and the silence before a sound; Dune, a wall of dust that swallows the horizon. James Cameron: every cause seen before its effect, weight that is physically true, and people running at the camera with the catastrophe behind them.
Camera package — ARRI Alexa 65, large format, spherical primes, shallow focus on the medium shots, fine grain. Shots 1 and 2 are low and locked off. Shot 3 is handheld: the only handheld shot in the film.

## VOICE DNA (immutable)
No one speaks or cries out. No word of any kind is heard.

## AUDIO DESIGN
Shots 1 and 2 are almost silent: a low wind and nothing else. The falling rock makes no sound at all, because its sound has not reached them yet. In shot 3: boots in mud, his breath, water slapping in the bowl; then the sound arrives, a deep rolling roar that grows and does not stop. No music, no drone.

[TIME] 7.0–12.0 s
[CAM] medium, frontal, chest height, 28 mm, HANDHELD, the camera running backward ahead of him down the mud path, shaking with every stride; the dark mass closes the top of the frame; behind him the black field and, where the mountain stood, a wall of pale dust coming on.
[BLOCK] The farmer runs straight at the camera, bent, his daughter carried level under his left arm with her face toward us, the bowl clutched to her chest. Far behind them a dozen valley people in the same grey wool run the same way from low stone houses, small and dark.

[DIALOGUE] / V.O. none

## PERFORMANCE ARC
THE FARMER: stillness → one step back → a dead run. He never straightens, and he never looks back.
THE DAUGHTER: watching → the bowl held tight → carried. She does not scream; her eyes stay wide and open.

## THEMATIC / SYMBOLIC / STRUCTURAL LOGIC
The fall is seen before it is heard, as lightning comes before thunder: the people above never hear it at all. She saves the water. Above, a gold cup of wine does not move.

## PHYSICS LOCKS
Real weight, real time, normal speed: no slow motion. The slab falls as rock falls, slowly at first and then fast, and breaks. Dust billows and rolls along the ground, slower than the fall. A stooped man carrying a six-year-old runs heavily, boots sinking in mud. Water slops out of the bowl.
