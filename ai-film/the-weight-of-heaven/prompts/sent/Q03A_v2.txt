# AUTEUR VIDEO PROMPT
## THE WEIGHT OF HEAVEN — Q03A · ANVIL VALE · BUILT TO BEAR · THE WATER
References: @Image 1 — character sheet: the FARMER · @Image 2 — character sheet: his DAUGHTER · @Image 3 — prop: the wooden bowl · @Image 4 — animal: the ox in its yoke · @Image 5 — environment: Anvil Vale, the place and its light
Reference mode · 16 s · 5 shots · 2.35:1 · photoreal live action

## SCENE INTENT
The world below, where everything carries. A bent farmer works his field under the dark underside of a floating kingdom; his daughter brings him water. She sees the water tremble before anyone knows why; then the animals know; then he sees dust open on the mountain. The quiet goes wrong one fact at a time, cause before effect, without a word.

## GLOBAL LOCKS
Character identity — The FARMER exactly as @Image 1: forties, weathered, short dark beard, his back bent by a life of carrying; he stoops even when standing and never straightens. His DAUGHTER exactly as @Image 2: six, small, one fair braid, grave grey eyes.
Wardrobe override — none: patched grey-brown wool exactly as the sheets, damp, wet earth on his hands.
Location / environment continuity — Anvil Vale exactly as @Image 5, and lit exactly as @Image 5: a low drystone wall across the foreground; black ploughed furrows beyond it to the horizon; on the right a low buttressed stone house, a mud path from its door to a gap in the wall; far off, left of centre, the flat-topped mountain, the Anvil; overhead the dark rock underside of the kingdom, a stone ceiling over the whole valley, and under it, along the horizon, one pale band of cold daylight. Overcast dusk, no sun, wet ground, a cold wind, low mist creeping over the furrows.
Props and temporal state — The bowl exactly as @Image 3, brimming with clear water. The ox exactly as @Image 4, yoked, roped to an iron ring in the wall. A long-handled hoe.

## MOVIE COLOR
One colour for this whole world: cold blue-grey slate. Black wet earth, grey stone, grey-brown wool, grass gone grey. The only bright thing in any frame is the pale band of daylight at the horizon, under the lid of the dark mass; the only warmth is skin and the wood of the bowl. Every shot is exposed for that band, so everything else sits a stop under: blacks deep, never crushed. People and animals are dark shapes against it; its cold light comes low from one side, rims a brow, a beard, a wet stone, and leaves the far side of each face in shadow. Mist does the rest: every plane further off is paler and softer than the one before it, and the mountain and the far reach of the mass overhead are half lost in it.
Influences, each with its purpose — Denis Villeneuve. Arrival: a band of light at the horizon under a lid of dark; a vast shape half lost in fog; the back of a head soft in the foreground and the thing it faces looming beyond. Dune: one colour to a world; a colossal underside over tiny figures; two people in profile, dark against an empty overcast sky. Blade Runner 2049: a farm under a dead grey sky; fog that swallows the distance; a profile sharp against a pale blur. Prisoners and Sicario: overcast exteriors exposed for the sky. James Cameron: every cause seen before its effect; weight that is physically true.
Camera package — ARRI Alexa 65, large format, spherical primes, wide open on every medium and close shot: shallow focus, soft backgrounds, gentle highlight roll-off, fine grain. The camera is low, at the height of a bent back, and locked off in every shot; the only movement is inside the frame.

## VOICE DNA (immutable)
No one speaks. No voice of any kind is heard.

## AUDIO DESIGN
Production sound only, close and dry, over a low steady wind on open ground. No music, no drone. In the last shot the wind dies almost to nothing.

## PERFORMANCE ARC
THE FARMER: labour → one swallow of rest → attention. Slow, sparing, tired; he never looks up at the mass overhead.
THE DAUGHTER: care not to spill → stillness → the first to know. She does not point, call out or step back.

## THEMATIC / SYMBOLIC / STRUCTURAL LOGIC
Weight made visible in water: this trembling bowl answers a gold cup of wine that stands perfectly still in the kingdom above, framed at the same size. Wall, buttress, yoke and back draw one bent line.

## PHYSICS LOCKS
Real weight, real time, normal speed. Mud grips boots and hooves. The full bowl is heavy in the hands. The water only shivers: it never slops or splashes. The rope holds. The crows cannot climb. Dust sifts downward.

## AUDIO LOCKS
No speech, whisper, singing, music or drone: only the wind and the sounds of what is on screen.
