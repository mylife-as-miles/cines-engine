## SCENE INTENT
This follows the sequence before directly. Serath has asked the Council of Orison for time. The Hierophant grants it, and in the same breath gives her command, should she fail, to her second, Cassiel: a man who waits by the door and is silent. His words are heard over the two people they dispose of, first her, then him. Only then is the Hierophant seen: he looks at her for the first time and says one warm thing. She takes it as a blessing, bows her head and goes. The order is sealed. Cassiel, who has waited for that, inclines his head to her as she passes. Every voice stays low.

## MOVIE COLOR
White stone in cloud, cool and grey: pale grey marble, grey-white cloud, white cloth. The gold of Serath's collar and the red of the sealing wax are the only colours. Every shot is exposed for the cloud in the arches: the room sits just under it, each face is lit softly from the side of the arches and falls off on the other side, and a face is never the brightest thing in the frame. Whites keep their detail. No cast shadows.
Influences, each with its purpose — Denis Villeneuve, Dune and Arrival: symmetry, stillness, shallow focus up close, one still figure held in a large frame, a hard thing heard on the face of the one it is said of. James Cameron: every look answered by what it looks at; cause, then effect, each readable alone: the seal, then the bow.
Camera package — ARRI Alexa 65, large format, spherical primes, fine grain. Shallow focus on faces. Every shot moves slowly and smoothly on a steady camera.

## VOICE DNA (immutable)
THE HIEROPHANT: The high speech at its purest: a liturgical Received Pronunciation, old-fashioned and round. A man of seventy-four with a rich, resonant bass-baritone, deep and full in the breath, a voice used to filling vast rooms without effort. Every sentence has a liturgical cadence and comes to rest on a falling end, like a pronouncement. He is perfectly steady and has never been in doubt.
No one else speaks. Serath and Cassiel are silent in every shot.

## AUDIO DESIGN
A quiet stone room open to the sky: soft moving air under everything. His voice is close and clear in shot 3, and comes from across the room in shots 1 and 2, with a short airy tail. Between the lines only air, breath, cloth and, at the end, her steps on the marble are heard.
