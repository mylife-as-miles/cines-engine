## SCENE INTENT
A small white naval cutter has come down beneath Orison, the kingdom that floats above the cloud. Now it flies out from under the kingdom's black underside, across a dead plain of black basalt, toward a black wall a mile long that stands across the horizon. One unbroken shot from the air, behind the cutter and travelling with it. It is slow and enormous: the cutter is the only white thing in the frame, and it is very small under what hangs over it.

Wardrobe override — none.
Location / environment continuity — Exactly @Image 1. Across the top of the frame, the black rock underside of the kingdom, running out of the frame, and beyond its far rim a band of pale grey daylight over low green hills. Under the hills, right across the frame, a straight black wall with nine tall arched gates: seven open to green daylight, two shut. Four iron chains hang plumb from the rock to the plain: three thin and far off, one thick, on the right. Below the wall the basalt plain: mist lying over it, stacks of black columns standing out of the mist to the left and right of a broad lane of mist that leads to the wall, and across the bottom of the frame, close beneath us, the tops of black six-sided columns.
Props and temporal state — The cutter exactly as it is in @Image 1 and exactly the design of @Image 2: a white wooden boat that flies, ten metres long, an open deck aft with a ship's wheel, a small cabin forward with pointed windows, a grey slate roof and a stubby white spire, brass rails, a rudder, and two dark iron landing skids under its keel. There is one cutter. It flies level, keel down, a few metres above the tops of the columns, with clear air and mist beneath it.

Influences, each with its purpose — Denis Villeneuve, Dune and Arrival: one small craft held in a very large, simple, hazy frame, every line leading to one far monument. James Cameron: size shown by a small known thing, a man in a boat, beside a huge one.

## THEMATIC / SYMBOLIC / STRUCTURAL LOGIC
The kingdom is a ceiling over someone else's country. The boat that comes from it is very small, and it is going to the one thing down here that holds the kingdom up.
