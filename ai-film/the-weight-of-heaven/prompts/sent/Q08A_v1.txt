Reference mode · 12 s · 3 shots · 2.35:1 · photoreal live action · no speech · no music

## SCENE INTENT
Three separate views, cut hard. In the yard of an old stone fort on the ground a white naval cutter has set down. Serath, an officer of the kingdom that floats above the cloud, has never stood on the ground. She steps onto it and for the first time in her life feels her own weight: her knees buckle and she almost goes down. Behind her, her aide falls outright. The people of the ground watch the sky people learn to stand. Nobody laughs. Then, close: the gold collar of her rank is heavy on her neck and pulls her head down, and she lifts her head against it. Last, elsewhere: the green valley that lies beyond the black wall, alive and unaware.

## MOVIE COLOR
The colour, light and haze of the three images, unchanged. The yard: cold grey and black, rock dust in the air; the cutter, Serath and the aide are the only white things, and the gold of her collar is the only colour. The valley: soft grey day, muted green. Exposed for the pale things; faces are lit softly from the left and are never the brightest thing in the frame. Blacks deep but never crushed.
Influences, each with its purpose — Denis Villeneuve, Arrival and Dune: the one who does not belong, sharp and pale among dark still watchers; a body told by its weight; stillness. James Cameron: a thing felt for the first time is shown on the body before the face.

## PERFORMANCE ARC
SERATH: steps down as she has stepped off a thousand decks → her body fails her for a second → she will not let it show → she holds her head up against the weight of her own rank, and looks at who is waiting for her.
THE AIDE: follows her → falls.
THE WATCHERS: still.

## THEMATIC / SYMBOLIC / STRUCTURAL LOGIC
Weight is real, and she has never felt it. The people who bear it all their lives watch her find out.
