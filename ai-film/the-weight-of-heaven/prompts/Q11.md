## Against the critics' standard (take B)

| Rule | Here |
| --- | --- |
| One light plan | One paragraph: cool daylight from the side each face looks to, the rest dark; the lamp a faint warm touch, never seen |
| Nobody looks into the lens | Every shot names where the eyes are: the bottom corner; up and to the left |
| Geography | Who is on which side, who is low and who stands, where the thing they look at lies |
| One coat, one face | Both from their sheets; Serath from the frame of the take before |
| No framing used twice | **Broken on purpose:** two set-ups, each used twice. It is a conversation between two faces, as the council is cut |
| A frame with size | **Not in this take.** The size is in the take before it (the hall with its floor of papers); this is the close of the scene |
| Fewer and longer | Four shots in 20 s |

## Decided here, for the author to overrule

- **The glove is not seen coming off.** The screenplay has "She takes off her glove and lays her bare hand on the shard." Two hands working a glove is asking for trouble; a bare hand where every hand before it was gloved says it. The white cuff of her sleeve is at the wrist.
- **Which hand** is not said in the prompt. The plan has her bare right hand here and the shard in her left from Q13 on.
