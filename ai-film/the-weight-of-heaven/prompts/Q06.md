## Why it is staged this way

- **His words fall on the people they dispose of.** "Until the bell." is heard on Serath, who asked. The sentence that takes her command away is heard on the man who will get it. The film has done this twice already ("Sarn Vale." on Serath's back, "Heaven has done your arithmetic for you" on her face), and the Dune scripts give the hard word to the listener.
- **Cassiel gets a face when he gets a name.** He has only been a figure by the door in the wide. He never speaks in the film and matters at the end of it; this is where the audience is told who he is. Held wide and still, as Villeneuve holds a figure; Cameron's rule that a look or a name is answered by what it points at.
- **The Hierophant is seen once, for the line that carries the film, and he faces forward.** "Remember who raised you" is his only line on screen here. He says it as he has said everything in the scene: frontal, centred, his head still (the shot list's own note: "warm, frontal"). The warmth is in his face and his voice. The draft had him turn his head to her; the author chose what fits what has been generated (26 November), and in Q04 and Q05 he never turns.
- **Four of nineteen words are on a visible mouth.** In Q04 he spoke five words in under a second on screen, and the author would not have him slowed afterwards. Off-screen words can also be re-timed or replaced in sound without touching a face.
- **She does not hear the warning.** One bow of the head, an officer's, and she goes. No reaction beyond that.
- **Her seal, then the bow.** The screenplay: "Cassiel waits for the seal before he inclines his head to her, perfectly courteous." The seal is Serath's own: her ring, her glove, red wax, as at the wedding (`PROP_P03`: "on every warrant"). The draft had the Council's seal in a clerk's hand; the author chose what fits what has been generated. It also keeps the film's spine: she seals this order as she sealed the others, and Karst will say "You sealed them." The last shot opens with Cassiel's eyes on the table, where she sealed it, before they come to her.
- **Three set-ups come back** (Serath's close shot, the Hierophant's close-up, the seal) and one new one is used twice (the door).
