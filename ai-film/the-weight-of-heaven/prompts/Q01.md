## Q01 — THE RITE: the wedding bell is hung and struck (B02, B06)

References: @Image 1 — environment: the Cathedral of the Ascension · @Image 2 — crowd: the wedding guests · @Image 3 — character sheet: the Hierophant · @Image 4 — casting: the bride · @Image 5 — casting: the groom · @Image 6 — prop: the marriage bell · @Image 7 — ship: the barge that lifts the bell · @Image 8 — prop: the bell's hammer

---

## SCENE INTENT

The kingdom of Orison crowns eight hundred years in the air with a royal wedding and a new bell. The Hierophant gives the creed, ten thousand answer it, and the bell the fleet lifted is given to the cathedral's beam: the kingdom takes its weight, and the bell is struck. By the last frame the audience must feel a world where nothing weighs anything, and must hear one heavy thing begin.

---

## GLOBAL LOCKS

**Character identity** — The Hierophant exactly as @Image 3: a man of seventy-four, light brown skin, a long face, deep-set dark eyes, a close-trimmed white beard. The bride exactly as @Image 4, the groom exactly as @Image 5. The guests as @Image 2: men, women and children of every age, every skin tone, all in white.
**Wardrobe override** — none: everyone exactly as their reference.

**Location / environment continuity** — The Cathedral of the Ascension exactly as @Image 1: honed white marble; columns no thicker than a man rising about two hundred feet into mist; balustraded galleries along both sides; tall open arches onto cloud; the open tower at the far end, open to the sky, with one stone beam across it. A clear central aisle. Day, cloud light.

**Props and temporal state** — The bell exactly as @Image 6, dark bronze; at the start it hangs in the tower still held in the four chains of the barge @Image 7, which hangs in the open sky above the tower; in shot 5 the chains fall slack and the bell settles onto the beam. The hammer exactly as @Image 8, on its white arm from the beam, beside the bell. Long cream and pale grey banners hang from the galleries. White petals in the air.

## MOVIE COLOR

THE WEIGHT OF HEAVEN, above: marble #E9E6DE in cloud light #F4F1EA, cloud #AEB4B8 in the mist and the soft greys between the columns; the bell's dark bronze the darkest thing in every frame. Cool, muted, felt, never saturated; whites hold their detail.

**Influences, each with its purpose** — Denis Villeneuve's scale: a small figure inside a huge frame so that size can be judged, symmetry, and the held wide. The method only, never a frame or a look from his films.

**Camera package** — ARRI Alexa 65, large format · 2.39:1 composition (generated 21:9) · spherical large-format primes, clean and high-resolving, flare-free · deep focus. Soft, low-contrast light from every side at once: diffuse daylight from the cloud through the arches and the open tower / daylight bounced off the marble, filling every shadow.

---

## VOICE DNA (immutable)

THE HIEROPHANT: The high speech at its purest: a liturgical Received Pronunciation, old-fashioned and round. A man of seventy-four with a rich, resonant bass-baritone, deep and full in the breath, a voice used to filling vast rooms without effort. Every sentence has a liturgical cadence and comes to rest on a falling end, like a pronouncement. He is perfectly steady and has never been in doubt. He speaks hard things gently, as a kindness.

THE TEN THOUSAND: A vast congregation answering in unison in a cathedral open to the cloud: men, women and children of every age. The response is murmured, not shouted, in an unhurried liturgical cadence. It sounds like habit, not passion, and the nave carries it into a long tail. It is the high speech in thousands of mouths at once.

---

## AUDIO DESIGN

The cathedral's long, soft, bright reverberation; air moving through open arches; petals and cloth silent. The Hierophant's voice carries without effort; the response blooms into the nave's tail. In shot 5 only the iron of the chains. At the very end, one deep bell stroke. No music: the film's song is laid in the edit.
