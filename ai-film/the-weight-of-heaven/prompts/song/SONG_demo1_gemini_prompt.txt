Create one full song, about three minutes long.

Style: Dark cinematic pop anthem, sung, modern, made for a fast film montage. 84 BPM, D minor, heavy half-time. Giant tribal drums, stomps and claps, deep sub bass, low brass blasts, tense staccato low strings. Under everything a deep throat-like male drone; far off, a wordless woman's wail. Lead vocal: a low, raw, intimate female alto, close to the microphone, unpolished, no autotune, opening to full power in the choruses. A crowd of low voices chants with her on the downbeats; a large choir answers like a congregation in a cathedral. It builds in steps and stops dead. Heavy, grieving, defiant. No electric guitar, no guitar solo, no EDM drop, no rap, no bright synths.

Sing exactly the lyrics below, in this order, every line, with nothing added. Words in round brackets are sung by the crowd of low voices as an answer to the lead.

Lyrics:

[Intro]
What heaven has raised,
let no hand bring down.

[Verse 1]
Petals hang up there like they forgot the ground,
down here the water's shaking in the bowl.
You pressed your stars in wax and didn't look;
we felt it in our backs before the toll.

[Pre-Chorus]
Every bell you lift into the light,
somewhere a mountain —

[Verse 2]
We built it wide, we built it low,
we learned to walk the way you lean;
eight hundred years we held the shade
of gardens we have never seen.
(Hold it up) we held it up,
(hold it up) we held it up.

[Bridge]
Open the doors (open the doors),
light every lamp (light every lamp),
count every road, count every bed,
raise up your hand —

[Chorus]
Let no hand bring down
what heaven raised so high,
a thousand ships, a thousand guns,
all waiting on one sign —
let no hand —

[Final Chorus]
Then let it come down,
a hand's breadth at a time;
lay down the gold, lay down the guns,
let every anchor sound.

[Outro]
Come down, come down,
we would have you come down.
Come down, come down —
