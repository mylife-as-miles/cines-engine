Dark cinematic pop anthem, sung, modern, made for a fast film montage. 84 BPM, D minor, heavy half-time. Giant tribal drums, stomps and claps, deep sub bass, low brass blasts, tense staccato low strings. Under everything a deep throat-like male drone; far off, a wordless woman's wail. Lead vocal: a low, raw, intimate female alto, close to the microphone, unpolished, no autotune, opening to full power in the choruses. A crowd of low voices chants with her on the downbeats; a large choir answers like a congregation in a cathedral. It builds in steps and stops dead. Heavy, grieving, defiant. No electric guitar, no guitar solo, no EDM drop, no rap, no bright synths.
