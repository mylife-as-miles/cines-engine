## Why it is staged this way

- **The cup first, and the same cup.** The Visual Bible's water motif is one insert four times: bowl, cup, cup, bowl. The cut's still cup is a high three-quarter view (take Q02 t1), so shot 1's first frame is built from those very frames: the cup and the marble are the take's own pixels; only what stands behind the table is new. The tremble is the only change. Cameron's rule: the cause is seen before anyone explains it.
- **The number lands on the picture.** "Six hundred feet since dawn" is said over the wide, where the cloud that used to lie far below is coming in over the balustrade. The voice bible's first principle: the picture says it before the line does.
- **A man with his back to us** (Villeneuve): the Engineer is first seen small, dusty and alone in a white room, facing the people he must tell. It also keeps twelve of the 33 words off any mouth.
- **Two frontal faces** for the two that answer each other: the Bible frames the Hierophant "frontal and symmetrical, like the building", and the Engineer "in the middle of the room, with nothing to lean on".
- **The valley's name falls on Serath** (changed after the Dune study, below). His reluctance is on his face: he stops and looks at her. The name itself is heard over her back, and her head turns. She answers it in the first line of Q05.

## Voices

- **The voices will be the video model's.** In movement I that is the one thing that still read as synthetic. Q04 is the test of that.
- The Hierophant's paragraph is the text sent for Q01, word for word, so that the model has the same description of his voice. It may still not match.
- The Engineer's paragraph is new wording (see the table). The voice bible's own paragraph is kept there with a note until the author confirms.
- No pronunciation note is given for Unrisen, Weir or Sarn: a quoted word is spoken twice (Oren lesson 40). The take's words will be checked against the script.

## Known risks

| Risk | What protects against it |

| The voices sound synthetic, or the Hierophant does not sound like Q01 | Nothing in a prompt can; this take is the test |
| "Sarn Vale." is lost or clipped in the last shot (the last shot is the one most often squeezed) | It comes one second into a four-second shot. It is off-screen, so a missing or wrong reading can be replaced in sound with no lip-sync |
