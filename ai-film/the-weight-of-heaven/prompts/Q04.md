## Why it is staged this way

- **The cup first, and the same cup.** The Visual Bible's water motif is one insert four times: bowl, cup, cup, bowl. The cut's still cup is a high three-quarter view (take Q02 t1), so shot 1's first frame is built from those very frames: the cup and the marble are the take's own pixels; only what stands behind the table is new. The tremble is the only change. Cameron's rule: the cause is seen before anyone explains it.
- **The number lands on the picture.** "Six hundred feet since dawn" is said over the wide, where the cloud that used to lie far below is coming in over the balustrade. The voice bible's first principle: the picture says it before the line does.
- **A man with his back to us** (Villeneuve): the Engineer is first seen small, dusty and alone in a white room, facing the people he must tell. It also keeps twelve of the 33 words off any mouth, which is kind to lip-sync.
- **Two frontal faces, centred**, for the two that answer each other: the Bible frames the Hierophant "frontal and symmetrical, like the building", and the Engineer "in the middle of the room, with nothing to lean on".
- **The valley's name is on his face, and the turn of her head is silent.** His "longest pause in the film" is played, not cut round. Serath's turn is the last shot because the last shot of a generation is always the one squeezed (lessons 3, 16): it has no words, and Q05 opens on it anyway.

## Voices

- The two Voice DNA paragraphs are the voice bible's, unchanged, less one sentence each that quotes a line (a quoted word in a direction is spoken as an extra word: Oren lesson 40). The Hierophant's is the text sent for Q01.
- **The voices will be the video model's.** In movement I that is the one thing that still read as synthetic, and the model speaks faster than people do whatever the prompt says (Oren lessons 36, 39). Q04 is the test of that: if the voices fail, the picture can still serve under voices made elsewhere, but lip-sync onto them would be new, paid work.
- The Hierophant's voice may not match his voice in Q01: the model is given the same words about it and nothing else.

## Known risks

| Risk | What protects against it |
| --- | --- |
| The voices sound synthetic, or the Hierophant does not sound like Q01 | Nothing in a prompt can; this take is the test |
| Lines spoken fast, with the spare time left as gaps | Each line has behaviour before and after it, so a gap has something in it; the edit can tighten |
| The wrong mouth moves in shot 3 (two faces look at the lens while a man with his back to us speaks) | "The others are still and their lips stay closed" |
| An unusual word is mispronounced (Weir, Sarn, Unrisen) | No pronunciation note is given, because a quoted word is spoken twice; if one is wrong, that line is replaced in sound |
| The opening holds long and squeezes the end | Two spare seconds; the last shot is silent and Q05 opens on it |
| Five faces from five sheets drift | Each is alone in its close shot; the wide is small |
| The first frame's faults are copied (lesson 51) | Checked at full size: the cup and marble are untouched take pixels; the Chancellor behind is soft |
