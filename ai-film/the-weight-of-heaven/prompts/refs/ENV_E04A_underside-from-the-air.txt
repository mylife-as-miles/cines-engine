# THE WEIGHT OF HEAVEN — ENVIRONMENT REFERENCE PROMPT

## BENEATH ORISON — THE UNDERSIDE, FROM THE AIR (shot plate)

Create a photorealistic cinematic environment reference image for BENEATH ORISON — THE UNDERSIDE OF THE KINGDOM.

This is a FILM PRODUCTION LOCATION REFERENCE, not concept art, not an architectural visualization, not advertising photography.

The environment must feel physically real, inhabited, spatially coherent, and suitable for repeated use as a continuity reference across multiple generated film shots.

---

# REFERENCE IMAGES

@Image 1 — the approved view beneath Orison, drawn from the ground. Use it for: the underside of the kingdom (raw dark rock in inverted crags, hanging earth and roots) and the iron chains, each link the size of a house.
@Image 2 — the approved basalt plain, drawn from the ground. Use it for: the black six-sided basalt of the plain.

Take from each reference only what it is listed for. Do not copy its framing, its camera position, its ground-level view, or the valley, the wall and the buildings in it.

---

# LOCATION IDENTITY

LOCATION:
The air beneath the Kingdom of Orison, just under the cloud, above the basalt plain

COUNTRY / REGION:
The ground: the bearing-lands of the eleven nations, beneath the floating kingdom of Orison (an invented world, built from real northern volcanic and upland country: columnar basalt, slate, dry-stone, heavy soil). Above: the underside of the floating kingdom, whose impossible scale is explicitly required.

SETTING:
aerial exterior: the raw rock underside of a floating kingdom, over a black volcanic plain

TIME PERIOD:
Year 800 of the Ascension. Pre-industrial: stone, bronze, brass, glass, oak, iron, chain, cloth, paper and wax. No electricity, no electric light, no radio, no engines visible, no plastic, no concrete.

SOCIAL / ECONOMIC CHARACTER:
The side of the kingdom no one above has ever seen.

OVERALL FEEL:
Not beautiful. Enormous, raw, and slowly sinking.

The location should feel like a place that existed before the camera arrived.

It must not feel production-designed for symbolism.

---

# SPATIAL GEOGRAPHY

Establish clear, believable geography, in three horizontal bands.

Include:

- the top half of the frame and more: the underside of the kingdom, a ceiling of raw black rock that runs out of the frame at the top, the left and the right, with no end, edge or outline anywhere; its far reaches dissolve into grey haze
- its surface as in @Image 1: inverted crags, hanging earth and roots
- three chains only, as in @Image 1, hanging plumb from the rock: one near, at the left third of the frame, enormous, black and sharp, cut off by the top of the frame, each link the size of a house; two farther off, each a step paler and softer, the farthest almost lost in haze
- under the rock, one narrow band of pale daylight across the whole frame, a sixth of the frame's height, the brightest thing in the picture
- under the band, the black basalt plain of @Image 2, seen from high above and far off: flat, dark, its columns too small to count, sheets of pale ground mist lying on it
- on the right third, a bank of grey cloud hangs below the rock into the band of light, lit from behind
- clear empty air in the middle of the band of light: nothing in it
- camera position: in the air, far from everything, level, two hundred metres under the rock and three hundred above the plain, looking along between them

There is no sea, no water, no hill, no coast, no valley, no wall and no building anywhere in this view: only rock above, light between, plain below.

# LIGHTING DESIGN

LIGHT ABOVE, HEAVY BELOW.
A LOW BRIGHT BAND UNDER A BLACK ROOF. HAZE BETWEEN.

Primary motivated source:

one band of pale daylight at the horizon, between the black rock above and the black plain below; it is the brightest thing in the frame and everything is exposed for it

Secondary motivated source:

that same daylight scattered in haze and ground mist: shafts of pale light slanting down through the haze, mist glowing where the band meets the plain

Maintain:

- deep shadow with retained detail: the rock and the plain sit two stops under the band
- strong separation of near and far by haze: near things black and sharp, far things pale, soft and low in contrast
- gentle highlight roll-off in the band
- believable exposure, as a large-format film camera exposed for the bright band

No fill light. No light from above. No sun disc. No blue sky.

# CAMERA / OPTICAL LANGUAGE

CAMERA:
ARRI Alexa 65, large format, from the air, steady, level horizon

ASPECT RATIO:
21:9

ANAMORPHIC CHARACTER:
none: spherical

LENS CHARACTER:
a long lens, 135 mm: the far things are pressed close behind the near ones; shallow focus is not noticeable at this distance; no wide-angle distortion

A frame from a feature film photographed with restraint: exposed for the highlights, low in contrast in the distance, one colour (cold blue-grey slate), fine grain. Not a matte painting, not concept art, not a game render.

---

# COMPOSITION

One frame of a film, made to be held on screen: not a master reference.

- three bands: black rock (the top 55 %), a band of light (the next 15 %), black plain (the bottom 30 %)
- the picture is mostly dark; the eye goes to the band of light and stays there
- a great deal of empty space; few things, each very large and very simple
- the near chain on the left third gives the size of everything: a link the size of a house
- level and still; no tilt, no dramatic angle

Scale: a ten-metre boat in the empty middle of the band of light would be a fleck, one thirtieth of the frame's width. Do not draw the boat.

---

# ATMOSPHERE

Heavy air. Depth is told in layers of haze: the nearest thing black and sharp, each thing behind it a step paler, the farthest almost lost. Ground mist lies on the plain in sheets and in the low lanes between the columns. Grey cloud hangs against the rock above. It is cold, still and vast. Nothing here was made to be looked at.

---
