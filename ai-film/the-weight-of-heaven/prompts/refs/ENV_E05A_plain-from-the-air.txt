# THE WEIGHT OF HEAVEN — ENVIRONMENT REFERENCE PROMPT

## THE BASALT PLAIN, FROM THE AIR (shot plate)

Create a photorealistic cinematic environment reference image for THE BASALT PLAIN.

This is a FILM PRODUCTION LOCATION REFERENCE, not concept art, not an architectural visualization, not advertising photography.

The environment must feel physically real, inhabited, spatially coherent, and suitable for repeated use as a continuity reference across multiple generated film shots.

# LOCATION IDENTITY

LOCATION:
The plain beneath Orison: black basalt to the horizon, where no one lives

COUNTRY / REGION:
The ground: the bearing-lands of the eleven nations, beneath the floating kingdom of Orison (an invented world, built from real northern volcanic and upland country: columnar basalt, slate, dry-stone, heavy soil). A lava plain from an eruption eight hundred years ago: "it was stone within forty years".

SETTING:
a vast, dead volcanic plain of columnar basalt

TIME PERIOD:
Year 800 of the Ascension. Pre-industrial: stone, bronze, brass, glass, oak, iron, chain, cloth, paper and wax. No electricity, no electric light, no radio, no engines visible, no plastic, no concrete.

SOCIAL / ECONOMIC CHARACTER:
No one lives here. Neutral ground between the eleven nations and the black wall.

OVERALL FEEL:
Black, stone, empty, old. A city no one finished.

The location should feel like a place that existed before the camera arrived.

It must not feel production-designed for symbolism.

# SPATIAL GEOGRAPHY

Establish clear, believable geography, seen from the air.

Include:

- the lower three quarters of the frame: the basalt plain of @Image 1 seen from three hundred metres up, looking forward and down: tens of thousands of black six-sided column tops packed edge to edge like a honeycomb, a pattern that runs unbroken to the far edge of the plain
- taller stacks of columns stand up out of the pattern here and there, small from this height, like the stumps of towers, each ringed with pale mist
- rivers of pale mist lie in the lower lanes between the columns and catch the light
- along the far edge of the plain, a thin straight black line from one side of the frame to the other: the wall of @Image 2, a mile long and thirty metres high, tiny with distance, nine small round arches in it, most of them glowing green and pale with the valley beyond
- above the wall, a narrow band of pale daylight
- the top eighth of the frame: the black underside of the kingdom as in @Image 3, a ceiling running out of the frame, two or three chains hanging from it into the haze, far off
- camera position: in the air, three hundred metres above the plain, level, looking forward toward the wall and thirty degrees down

There is no foreground object, no boat, no person, no road and no building on the plain: only the columns, the mist and the wall.

# LIGHTING DESIGN

LIGHT ABOVE, HEAVY BELOW.
A LOW BRIGHT BAND UNDER A BLACK ROOF. HAZE BETWEEN.

Primary motivated source:

one band of pale daylight at the horizon, between the black rock above and the black plain below; it is the brightest thing in the frame and everything is exposed for it

Secondary motivated source:

that same daylight scattered in haze and ground mist: shafts of pale light slanting down through the haze, mist glowing where the band meets the plain

Maintain:

- deep shadow with retained detail: the rock and the plain sit two stops under the band
- strong separation of near and far by haze: near things black and sharp, far things pale, soft and low in contrast
- gentle highlight roll-off in the band
- believable exposure, as a large-format film camera exposed for the bright band

No fill light. No light from above. No sun disc. No blue sky.

The light comes from the band of day, low and from ahead: it rakes across the column tops, so that each has one thin pale edge toward the light and the pattern shows; the mist in the lanes glows.

# COMPOSITION

One frame of a film, made to be held on screen: not a master reference.

- seen from the air, never from the ground: no column is closer to the camera than three hundred metres
- the plain is a pattern that fills the frame; the wall is one thin line across it; the band of light and the black ceiling close the top
- the picture is mostly dark, with pale mist drawing lines through it toward the wall
- level and still; the far edge of the plain is straight and horizontal

Scale: the wall is a mile long and looks no thicker than a pencil line; a ten-metre boat flying over the plain would be a fleck. Do not draw the boat.

---

# ATMOSPHERE

Heavy air. Depth is told in layers of haze: the nearest thing black and sharp, each thing behind it a step paler, the farthest almost lost. Ground mist lies on the plain in sheets and in the low lanes between the columns. Grey cloud hangs against the rock above. It is cold, still and vast. Nothing here was made to be looked at.
