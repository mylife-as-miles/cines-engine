# THE WEIGHT OF HEAVEN — CASTING REFERENCE

## KARST

Create a photorealistic casting reference photograph of an original character for the feature film THE WEIGHT OF HEAVEN. A real-looking person photographed in costume, not a render, not a painting, not concept art. The person is invented and must not resemble any real actor or public figure.

LAYOUT
One landscape image, two panels side by side on one continuous neutral mid-grey (#808080) seamless background, with no divider line, border or gap. LEFT PANEL: the whole figure standing straight, facing the camera, arms relaxed at the sides, from the top of the head to the footwear, with a little space above and below. RIGHT PANEL: the same person, head and shoulders, facing the camera, eyes to the lens, a serious neutral expression. The same person, the same costume, the same light in both panels.

WHO
An engineer of the ground in his mid-fifties, once the empire's own; he drew where every warrant would fall. Too tired to be angry; he has done the sums.

WORLD
the ground beneath Orison, the bearing-lands of the eleven nations (an invented world, Year 800 of the Ascension; no electricity). Ground people are broad and bare-handed; everything they wear is undyed, worn and mended, and they have carried weight all their lives.

FACE AND BODY
A man of fifty-six with medium-brown South Asian skin; a long, tired face, heavy-lidded dark eyes, deep lines from nose to mouth; a short grey-black beard.

HAIR
Thinning black hair gone grey, cut short, uncombed.

WARDROBE
A long engineer's coat that was once white imperial wool, now worn to the grey-cream of rock dust, mended at the elbows and the hem. On the chest and on the collar, clean patches of brighter cloth where the insignia were cut away, the stitch holes still showing. A plain undyed shirt, dark wool trousers, worn and mended leather boots.

HANDS
Bare. Ink stains on the fingers of the right hand and under the nails.

BEARING
Slightly stooped, still, watchful; a man who stands at doorways. For this reference, they stand straight and neutral.

CONDITION
Worn, mended, dusty at the hem.

LIGHT AND CAMERA
Soft, even, low-contrast studio light from a large diffused source with gentle fill; true skin tone and real skin texture (pores, lines, small marks); neutral white balance. Full-frame camera, about 50 mm for the figure and 85 mm for the portrait; deep focus; sharp throughout.

COLOUR
The film's palette, for colour only: marble #E9E6DE, cloud #AEB4B8, slate #4B5258, rock dust #8A857B, basalt #141517; gold #C8A45A belongs only to Serath's collar and the cup. Cool and muted.

NEGATIVE
No text, labels, captions, names, numbers or typography anywhere. No props unless listed above. No jewellery unless listed. No glamour make-up, no fashion styling, no fantasy or game costume styling, no cosplay. No medals, badges, logos or emblems unless listed. No other people. No resemblance to any real person.
