## Why it is staged this way

- **The Council never turns round to her.** The Hierophant and the Chancellor stand at the table with Serath behind them at her arch. He answers her over his shoulder without moving his head; only the Chancellor turns, and only to give the order. It keeps every speaking face to the camera, and it says who decides and who carries.
- **"Heaven has done your arithmetic for you" falls on Serath's face.** The first half of his answer is on him; the kindness is heard over her, as "Sarn Vale." was heard over her back in Q04 (the Dune scripts give the hard word to the listener). It also keeps seven of his eleven words off his mouth: in Q04 he spoke five words in under a second, and the author would not have him slowed afterwards.
- **Serath's close shot is one set-up used three times** (her line, her listening, her answer), so the film can cut back to her as to a fixed point.
- **Her answer waits.** The voice bible: her feeling shows "in how long she waits before she answers".
- **Shot 1 is the join.** It opens on Q04's last frame and turns her to the room. The edit can run it straight on from Q04, or cut from Q04 to shot 2 and leave it out; it costs two seconds and fixes where she stands and how she looks.

## Voices

- **Serath speaks for the first time in the film here**, and the Chancellor too. Their voice paragraphs are the voice bible's, cut down to sound only (accent, age, pitch, pace, manner): the sentences that quoted lines or described habits are left out, as for the Engineer in Q04 (lesson 72). **For the author to confirm**, Serath's above all: she has fourteen lines in the film and this take sets her voice.
- The Hierophant's paragraph is word for word the one sent for Q01 and Q04.
- **His voice may not match Q04's.** Nothing in a prompt can hold a voice from one generation to the next. fal does accept an audio reference for a voice (`audio_urls`, each file 1.8 to 30.2 s, no extra charge stated), but Q04 holds only one second of him, its documentation speaks of "lip-syncing to one audio reference", which could put his Q04 line into Q05, and the author's rule is images only. Not used. If his voice comes back different, the remedy is in sound (his seven off-screen words can be replaced without lip-sync) or a retake.
