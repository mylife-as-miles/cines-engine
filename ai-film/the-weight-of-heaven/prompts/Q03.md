## Known risks

- **The landslide** is the hardest thing asked of the model so far: a whole cliff face breaking away, far off, with dust. It is alone in the 7 s generation so a miss costs $1.80.
- **Crows that cannot climb** is an unusual motion; the model may let them fly off.
- **The daughter's eyes just above the wall** depend on her height against the wall; the block-out sets it, the model may show more or less of her face.
- **The dark mass overhead** must read as land, not cloud: the location reference carries it.
- **Sound:** the model may still add music; if it does, the sound is replaced in the mix at no fal cost.
