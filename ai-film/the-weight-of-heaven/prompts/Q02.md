## SCENE INTENT

At the wedding, Grand Admiral Serath catches a falling basket without looking and seals the day's lifting warrant without reading it. The aide lifts the sealed warrant to the tower, the barge lets the new bell go onto the cathedral's beam, and the kingdom takes its weight; a hammer the size of a door strikes it. Above, a cup of wine stands perfectly still. The audience must see one gloved hand sign for a weight it never feels.

---

**Wardrobe override** — none: everyone exactly as their reference. Serath wears the gold collar closed and the seal ring over her right glove.

**Location / environment continuity** — the cathedral exactly as @Image 2, and as @Image 1 for the first shot. The admirals stand at the front of the congregation on the nave floor, left of the aisle; the rite at the foot of the open tower. Day, cloud light.

**Props and temporal state** — The basket full of white petals, in the girl's two hands. The warrant (@Image 8) in the aide's hands, with soft red wax at its foot; after shot 4 it bears her seal. The bell (@Image 10) at first just above the stone beam in the barge's four chains; after shot 5 it rests on the beam and the chains hang slack. The hammer (@Image 12) at rest, then drawn back, then striking. The cup (@Image 13) on a gallery balustrade, its wine perfectly still.

THE WEIGHT OF HEAVEN, above: marble #E9E6DE in cloud light #F4F1EA, cloud #AEB4B8. The bell's dark bronze is the darkest thing. Three accents, small and alone: the gold #C8A45A of her collar and of the cup, and the seal red #9E2A22 of the wax (its first appearance in the film). Felt, never saturated.
