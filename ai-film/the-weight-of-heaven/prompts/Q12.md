## Decided here, for the author to overrule

- **One phrase is reworded.** The screenplay has "And the bearing-lands are no longer yours." Nobody in the film has yet said "bearing-lands": the lines that explained it were trimmed. The prompt has "And the lands that carry you are no longer yours.", which needs no explaining and answers "We stopped carrying it." and "That will carry you." Say the word and it goes back to the screenplay's.
- **Two takes, not one.** As above.
- **The glove is not seen going on.** The screenplay ends the beat with "she puts her glove back on." Two hands working a glove is asking for trouble. Offered instead, in the edit and at no cost: the hand take the film has, played backwards, so that her bare hand lifts off the shard and is gone (`out/edits/Q11H_t1_lift.mp4`, 3.5 s; checked in stills only). It is in no cut until the author says so. In Q13 she is gloved again and picks the shard up.
- **No frame holds them both.** The reel's over-the-shoulder two-shot is not in these takes (see the review). It can be bought alone after take B.
- **His move to the opening is not seen.** Take A opens with him already there.
- **The plain is empty and the sky white.** No cities overhead: Orison's underside is for the scenes that are about it.
