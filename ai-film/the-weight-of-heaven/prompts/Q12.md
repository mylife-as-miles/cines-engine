## Against the critics' standard

| Rule | Here |
| --- | --- |
| One light plan | One source, named and placed: the opening in the end wall. He stands at it, so from behind he is a dark shape and from the front his face is in shade; she faces it. This is how Q11 came back, which was right where its prompt was not (lesson 179) |
| A frame with size | B · 4: both of them, the opening, the stone. Four seconds, the take's last, nothing spoken on it |

| Geography | A paragraph in each prompt: he at the opening, she a few steps inside and low; Karst on the left looking right, Serath on the right looking left, as in Q11 |
| A back against the sky is a silhouette | A · 1 and B · 4 are written so |
| One coat, one face | Both from their sheets and from frames of the film |
| No framing used twice | **Broken on purpose**, as in Q11: two singles, each returned to. A · 1 returns to Q10 take 2's view of him at the opening, as a parallel |
| Fewer and longer | Six shots in 32 s; nothing under 3.6 s |

## Decided here, for the author to overrule

- **Two takes, not one.** As above.
- **The glove is not seen going on.** The screenplay ends the beat with "she puts her glove back on." Two hands working a glove is asking for trouble, and the hand's first take showed how the model treats a hand's last moment. Offered instead, in the edit and at no cost: the hand take the film has, played backwards, so that her bare hand lifts off the shard and is gone (the board's last frame). It is not in either take and not in any cut until the author says so. In Q13 she is gloved again and picks the shard up.
- **B · 4 is optional.** Without it take B is 15 s and $3.31. It is the one new set-up; it is also the only frame in either talk that holds both of them.
- **"He turns back to the plain" is not seen as a turn.** Take A opens with him already turned. A turn is an action with nothing to say; the cut from her look to his back says it.
- **The stone outside is empty and the sky white.** No cities overhead in the opening: Orison's underside is for the scenes that are about it.

## Known risks

| Risk | What protects against it |
| --- | --- |
| A line is lost or replaced | Four phrases a take; references in the number that has held; no first frame; every line on camera; each phrase with its own seconds; a silent stretch ends each take; the directions read through for anything that could be said |
| A · 1 comes back as Q10 take 2's shot, head bowed | "his head up", "he looks straight out"; if it does bow, the shot still works |
