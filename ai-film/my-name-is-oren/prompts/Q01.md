## SCENE INTENT

The film opens on a street that runs itself. Three kerb sweepers work the gutter, two city units empty a bin in identical grey regulation shoes, and the laundrette window offers free help beside an older, hand-made kindness, half peeled away. Then we find Helena the way this film finds everyone: by her feet. Her boots are failing, her hand is flat on the small of her back, and on a cracked handset she is drawing her own boots. By the last frame we know her before she has said a word: hurting, broke, proud, still drawing.

Two grammars: the city locked-off and symmetrical, Helena handheld and breathing.
