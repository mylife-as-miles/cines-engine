## THEMATIC / SYMBOLIC / STRUCTURAL LOGIC

The bag is the third person in the conversation. When Helena can't say something, her eyes find it (the script's own move), and the camera shows what she sees once, for two seconds, then goes back to her face. The verbs turn in three steps: she confides, she takes it back, he sets the line. He refuses without ever saying no, because his loyalty is to someone who isn't there. His glance down at the bag is the same glance that, in B25, will be the only sign of his conflict.
