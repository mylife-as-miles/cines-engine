# My Name Is Oren: shots (draft)

> **Superseded timing.** This first draft runs 9:13 before trims and predates `BEATS.md`. Both contests cap entries at 7:00, so the shot list will be re-cut to the beats' 6:45 target.

This draft is cut from the screenplay. The timings are estimates until the table read; the shots will change once the look and the faces are settled. Each **Q** is one generated sequence and one auteur prompt (`prompts/Qnn.md`), at most 15 s. Q00 and Q42 are made in code.

**Rules for every shot.** These are proposals in `DECISIONS.md` until the author settles them:
- **Feet first.** Pavement level until faces are earned. The first eye-level two-shot is Q30.
- **Helena handheld, Oren locked-off.** On the walk (Q40–Q41) the camera moves at her pace.
- **One axis.** The camera stays on the street side, with the laundrette and the low wall behind them. The corner and the clinic are frame right, and the walk runs left to right.
- **Oren's left side** (seam, temple light) is the camera side whenever he faces Helena.
- **No readable text from the model.** Decals, screens and the super come in the finish.

**Continuity line (the whole film).** Overcast early afternoon, 1:31 to about 1:45 pm, a damp pavement and no rain falling. Helena wears the olive field jacket, the grey hoodie and the boots with the loose left sole. Oren wears the navy fleece and the slippers with blue tape on the left one. The bag goes from his right hand (Q01–Q16) to his left (Q17–Q22), sits between his feet (Q23–Q31), goes back to his right (Q32–Q37), and ends in his left with his right arm offered (Q38–Q41).
