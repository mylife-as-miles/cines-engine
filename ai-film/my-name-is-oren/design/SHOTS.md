# My Name Is Oren: generation sequences (Seedance 2.5, 20 s each)

The film's **32 beats** (`BEATS.md`) become **22 generations of 20 s each** (the author's choice), covering B02–B31. B01 (over black) and B32 (the title card) are made in post. Each generation has one auteur prompt (`prompts/Qnn.md`).

**For every generation:**
- **Duration 20 s.** The content planned inside runs 14–20 s and never more; the rest is edit room, because the slow first and last half-second are trimmed.
- **Splits:** sequences break at the end of a beat where possible. Where a beat had to be split, the split falls between two lines, never inside one (listed below).
- **Draft at 480p**, choose a take, then make the final from that draft.
- **Start frames:** where action runs across a cut, the last frame of one generation is the start frame of the next.
- **The prompt** carries the MOVIE COLOR block and the Voice Bible unchanged (`DECISIONS.md`), and the performance comes from `DIRECTION.md`.

**Running time:** 6:28 of planned content, plus B01 (5 s) and B32 (6 s), is **6:39**, which is 21 s under the contests' 7:00 cap.

## What every prompt applies (learnt from Q01 t1)

- **21:9 on every generation.** Check the delivered size, because 16:9 loses 12% to the 2.35:1 matte.
- **A detail the story needs must read on screen.** Hold it 1.5–2 s, near the centre of frame, named in the prompt as clearly visible, with which side it is on ("the RIGHT boot's sole, open at the toe").
- **Contact is explicit.** "Hovers 1 cm above, never touches"; "goes fully into the pocket, the corner catching the lining".
- **One idea per shot, and no more than six shots in 20 s.**

## Order of generation

1. **Pilot: Q10.** It tests both faces, Oren's temple light, both voices, the handset plate and the start of a hero performance (her rank; her head comes up).
2. **The hero performances next, because they're the riskiest:** Q11 (being seen), Q13 (the laugh), Q18 (the doors), Q22 (the blink and the corner).
3. **Then the rest in film order**, chaining start and end frames.
