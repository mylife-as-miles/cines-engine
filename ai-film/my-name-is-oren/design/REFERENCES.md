# My Name Is Oren: references to make

The film's continuity, made before any video, from the author's templates in `.claude/skills/cines-engine/assets/ai-film/`. The environment and prop templates carry *YELLOW*'s palette; for this film, that block is swapped for this film's bible (`DECISIONS.md`). Each reference gets two to four candidates on a board, and the author chooses. The filled prompt and the chosen job id go in `prompts/refs/<ID>.md`. Every sequence cites the chosen files as @Image N.

## Order of work

1. **Style frames**: looks A, B and C, each drawn as the same three moments (9 images). The author picks a look.
2. **Casting**: three or four portraits each of Helena and Oren, in the chosen look (or the author's own consented reference photos).
3. **Sheets**: characters, environments and props below, from the chosen portraits and the chosen look.
4. **Hero keyframes**: one per scene beat (about 8), built from the sheets. They become start frames.
