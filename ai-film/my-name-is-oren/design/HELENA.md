# Feeling and understanding Helena

The author asked us to learn to feel and understand Helena before we score her, cut her or generate her again (2026-11-25). Her inner life comes from the author's short story, which **she tells herself** (`design/story.txt`). The screenplay (`design/script.json`) stays the source of every line and event; the story is what she knows and never says. Her body is in `DIRECTION.md` (Helena: physical vocabulary); this file is what is happening inside it.

## 1. Who she is, in her own words

- **Second Lieutenant Helena Duncan.** Two tours. "Came home with a hole in my back." The wound is in her back, "but it gets me down the leg". There were pills, then different pills, then appointments about the pills, then an assessment upstairs in a building whose lift was broken. They wrote that she didn't attend, and her benefits were cut.
- **She is hungry, and probably has nowhere to live.** "Yesterday was a little difficult to account for." Later: "They'll want an address."
- **Her war is still in her.** Her night helper "stayed talking when the noise outside turned into something else in my head."
- **She reads the city by its shoes.** "The city ones all wore the same shoes." Oren wears big ugly slippers with straps, one mended with blue tape.
- **She is kind to machines.** She asked her handset to answer to "shithead" once, in a bad week, and "felt awful when it did". She never knows what to call them: "Man. Buddy. You."
- **The people were the ones who hurt her.** At the intake desk: "Lieutenant Duncan, your appointment was yesterday. Lieutenant Duncan, we need you to remain calm." Her rank has been used against her.
- **Four helpers live on her cracked handset:** one for forms, one for appointments, one that talks her through bad nights, and one "meant to help me draw". On that one: "half a page of boots saved on it. Mine, mostly. I never got the laces right."
- **She has been to the clinic before.** "Last time I got as far as the doors. Stood there with my hand on the plate till somebody asked if I was going in. I said no."
- **Her father** "kept waiting for the next big one". The future he waited for got cheaper instead.

## 3. Where the feeling lives: her contradictions

She is proud and hungry; funny and in pain; a soldier who draws boots. She is polite to machines that can't hurt her, wary of the people who did, and still she wishes for a person at the desk. She is capable, and she cannot walk through a door.

**Every scene should hold two of these at once.** That is what makes her feel real before we understand her, and it is why the film never asks us to pity her.

## 4. The spine: names

- He says "My name is Oren" twice and gives her nothing else to call him; she calls him "buddy". At first, "recognising you as an individual" is a joke.
- He calls her by her rank. It returns her dignity, and it stings, because the desk used that rank to dismiss her.
- She gives him her name: "Helena will do." He takes it: "All right, Helena."
- Her last line is **"Slow down, Oren."** For the first time she uses his name, and she sets the pace. Then the title: **MY NAME IS OREN.**

The film is two people giving each other their names. The music's theme belongs to that, not to her suffering.

## 6. What she would hate, so the film never does it

Pity, swelling strings on her pain, being rescued, tears, speeches, congratulations before anything has happened.

- The score never feels sorry for her. It arrives for her **dignity**, her **choices** and her **humour**, never to underline her suffering.
- It stays dry where she is guarded. It warms only when she lets something in: the shoulder, her name, the laugh, his name.
- It goes silent where she protects herself: "Not the recordings."

## 7. What this changes now

- **M04 moves its entrance** from the share menu to the moment she presses Send (her choice to trust), about 3:43. The rising phrase on her rank keeps a suspension, not a triumphant resolution.
- **Choosing takes:** the drier, more restrained take of every cue wins. Nothing swells under her pain.
- **The theme is unfinished until the end.** The laces she "never got right", and "I can't even--", are left hanging. Each statement stops before its last note; M01c completes it after "Slow down, Oren."
- **For new generations** (with the author's go): her POV of the bag (A13), the camera at her eye level once she sits, handheld for her only, and wordless reactions over explanations. Two contradictions in every beat.
