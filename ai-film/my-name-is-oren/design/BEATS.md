# My Name Is Oren: beats

The screenplay is split into **32 beats in seven movements**. A beat is one change: something happens, and something is different after it.

**Script ids** point to `design/script.json`, which `tools/parse_script.py` builds from the screenplay: L01–L73 are the 73 lines of dialogue and A01–A58 the action. No dialogue is retyped.

**Timing.** At natural pace the film runs **7:42**. That figure comes from each line's word count at speaking pace (Helena 2.7 words a second, Oren an unhurried 2.3) plus time for each action. The **target is 6:45 including the title**, which leaves 15 s under the 7:00 cap that both contests set, for credits. The 57 s come out of pauses, action and quicker pickups, not dialogue. A table read will confirm the timings.

## The spine

Helena is starving and won't ask for the food in Oren's hand. Oren must deliver it by two o'clock. Everything he can offer is information or procedure, and none of it gets her through the clinic doors. What does is the one thing his client forbids: arriving late, at her pace.

**Names are the film's hidden structure.** Helena calls him "buddy" four times (L01, L14, L32, L45), and she says his name **only once**, in the climax: "Slow down, Oren." (L73). The client uses his name twice, as an order: "Two o'clock, Oren." Two people call him by name with opposite demands, and he answers Helena's. He uses her rank first, and then her name once she offers it ("Helena will do"). The title is the payoff: he told her his name twice, and she used it when it mattered.
