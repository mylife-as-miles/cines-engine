## THE GIRL

Create a photorealistic casting reference photograph of an original character for the short film THE FOREST ON THE WALL. A real-looking child photographed in costume, not a render, not a painting, not concept art. The child is invented and must not resemble any real person.

WORLD

FACE AND BODY
A real 7-year-old's height and proportions: small, slight, a child's round cheeks and small hands. An ordinary, natural, unretouched face with real skin texture. [AUTHOR: skin tone and features]

COLOUR

NEGATIVE
No text, labels, captions, names, numbers or typography anywhere. No props. No blanket, no toys. No jewellery. No make-up. No fashion styling, no posing, no cuteness exaggeration, no cartoon proportions. No other people. No resemblance to any real person.
