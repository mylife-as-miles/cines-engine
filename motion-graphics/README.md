# Motion Graphics

Product launches, ads and explainers: supers, captions, route and progress bars, data cards, product titles, end cards and disclaimers, designed in code (canvas pages rendered frame by frame, with alpha) and composited over footage or previs with ffmpeg, then voiced, scored and mixed.

Each job gets its own folder: `motion-graphics/<slug>/` with a `README.md`, `design/DECISIONS.md` (wording, claims and disclaimers the client approved), `gfx/` (the graphics pages), `tools/`, `audio/` and an ignored `out/`. Client footage and logos are not committed unless the client allows it; keep a note of where they live instead.

How to make one: `.claude/skills/cines-engine/references/motion-graphics.md`.
