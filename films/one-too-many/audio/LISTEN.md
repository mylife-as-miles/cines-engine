# Five places to check by ear

The checks below were measured, not heard. These are the moments where a listener should decide.

| Time | What to listen for |
| --- | --- |
| 0:23 to 0:31 | The cold open. The recorded house fire, the glass breaking inside, and then the smash to silence at 0:30.8. Check that the fire loop doesn't audibly repeat, and that the cut to silence is clean. |
| 2:42 | The party. The walla is a recorded restaurant ambience under the laughter recordings. Check that no words or music stand out from it, and that the laughs sit in the room rather than on top of it. |
| 6:00.8 | The kitchen crash: real pot and glass recordings, played by the engine at the film's designed level. They peak at -2 dBFS on the limiter. Check that the hit isn't harsh. |
| 10:10.5 | The scream: a real recording, about +8 LU over the room. Check that it reads as human and terrible rather than as a sound effect, and that it doesn't overpower the gas hiss that follows. |
| 12:37 | The explosion: a real recording layered with a sub boom, with the music cut dead 0.4 s before. It plays +10 LU over the room. Check its weight on small speakers. |
