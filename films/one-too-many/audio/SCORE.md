# The score

This is the plan for the score, which Lyria 3.5 generates (`tools/lyria.py`). There is one prompt per cue, and every time is on the film's clock.

## Sound world

ONE ROOM, TWO MUSICS.
(1) THE RECORD, the party's only music, always diegetic: a mellow neo-soul / lounge-jazz trio, recorded clean and close (a Fender Rhodes with soft amp tremolo, a round fingered electric bass, brushes and a soft felt kick). It is in 6/8 at 70 bpm on the dotted quarter, so one bar is 1.714 s, almost the 1.7 s beat of the final unison. Key: D minor (Dorian), Dm9 | G13 | Cmaj9 | A7(b9), with the Rhodes top voice falling E-E-D-B-flat. Where it is heard: through the wall (M03, low-pass 0.9 kHz), in the room (M04a, 8 kHz), through the doorway at the card table (M04b and M06a, 3.2 kHz), and as 'the next record' (M06b, 3.2 kHz): A minor, 7/8, one beat too many in every bar, with no G natural in it.
(2) THE SCORE, non-diegetic: a small, close, dry chamber string group in a small room (two to four cellos, two violas, two violins), muted (con sordino), no vibrato, mostly sul tasto. There is no double bass. The lowest score note is the cello's low C (65 Hz), and every underscore cue is high-passed at 75 Hz (P5), so the 55 Hz fundamental belongs to the Extra's drone alone. Three solo colours each have one job:
- a felt-muted upright piano: the count (M01, with its reprise in M12) and the numb call (M11b);
- a solo cello: grief (Deena's one held note in M10b; five falling notes for the five friends in M11b);
- the party's Rhodes, close and dry, used exactly once outside the records: one Dm9 at the jacket (M10d).
Dropped from the draft: bowed glass, bass clarinet, double bass, and the 'distant' Rhodes in the dining room, which read as the speaker switching itself back on.

RULES (so the music leaves room for the story's sounds):
- No drums, percussion, pulse, walking line or ostinato in any underscore. The heartbeat owns tempo, and the cards, slaps and pings own rhythm. The score never counts against the pings.
- No risers, braams, booms, hits, whooshes or trailer gestures. Every impact is a sound effect. Cues end on holes, hard cuts or designed silence.
- No choir, voices, synth pads, bells, celesta, chimes or glass.
- The top octaves stay empty: nothing above about 1.5 kHz in the underscore except a few single soft harmonics. The pings sit at 2.8-7.3 kHz, the gas hiss above 2.8 kHz and the tinnitus at 6.2 kHz.
- Everything is in tune at A = 440, measured and corrected to within 3 cents (P5). No instrument is asked to be 'detuned' or 'out of tune', because the souring belongs to the pings. The only wobble is a gentle tape wobble on the M10d Rhodes.
- The Extra's flat seventh (384.7 / 385.4 Hz) belongs to drone7. The score never plays a G in the middle register, so it never covers his partial or beats against it. The score's only Gs are a high G5 (the count's sixth note, and the top of Angelo's A7(b9)) and the kitchen's low lament bass on G2.
- Prompts name no sound effects, no violence and no existing works. Scenes are described only by feeling.

DYNAMICS:
- Underscore from Act 1 to Act 3 is ppp to p, capped at mp (M07's one step). There is no tremolo, sul ponticello or three-note cluster before the kitchen.
- M08b, at the fifth wineglass, is the first mf.
- The stove struggle (M10a) is the only passage at mf to f, and the first time the score presses (tremolo, ponticello, the A / B-flat / C cluster). The knife tremolo (M10c) stays low and steady.
- After the oven door nothing is louder than p. The standoff, the run and the blast have no score. The yard and the call are pp to p.

## Motifs

- **THE RUB (one too many)**: A against the B-flat a semitone above: the Phrygian flat second, and the flat ninth of the party's own A7(b9). It is the sound of a count that comes out one too many. It is always a held dyad (sometimes spread wide as a minor ninth, once doubled in three octaves), never a line and never a riser. It presses only once, in the kitchen, where it widens into the A / B-flat / C tremolo cluster. *Where it plays:* M05a: two violas on the seventh pile, the score's first word. M05b: with a low A beneath, from Christof's pause to 'Why?'. M07: A2 against B-flat3 through the corridor and the Christof logic, then up into the violins (A5 / B-flat5) when they look at the bottle, held on the doorway. M08a: the E of the held fifth gives way to B-flat after 'Didn't somebody already say me?'. M08b: in three octaves, the first mf. M10a: the only time it presses (sul ponticello tremolo on A / B-flat / C, forte at the oven handle). M11a: the B-flat inside the unresolved A7(b9). M11b: A3 and B-flat3 from 'Five. I had five over.' to the hole for '6'.
- **THE COUNT (one note too many)**: A felt-muted upright piano plays slow single notes about 1.3 s apart, each left to ring: E5, D5, C5, B4, A4, then a sixth note that does not belong, a high G5 a seventh above the A, left hanging. Both prompts use identical wording and register, so the reprise is recognised. The G5 is in tune and far above the drone's 385 Hz partial: it points at the Extra without playing his note. Its echo is the solo cello's five falling notes for the five friends. The score no longer counts alongside the pings; the draft's countable lines in M08a, M09 and M11c are removed. *Where it plays:* M01: under 'You said there were six people.' (from about 10.0). M12: under 'You're safe for now.' (from about 846.3). Echo: M11b's solo cello plays E, D, C, B-flat, A on the sparks (about 769.6-775.6), one note per friend.
- **THE RECORD AND ITS MEMORY (the friends' warmth)**: The party's record: Dm9 | G13 | Cmaj9 | A7(b9) on a Rhodes trio in 6/8 at 70 bpm, with the top voice falling E-E-D-B-flat. It is source music only while the party lives. Its turnaround chord, A7(b9), already holds the Extra's A, G and B-flat, and the film never lets it resolve. After the party dies it is remembered only twice, never as a 'distant speaker': once in the strings and once on a close, dry Rhodes. Its cadence returns as Angelo's goodbye. *Where it plays:* Source: M03 (through the wall), M04a (in the room), M04b (through the doorway), M06a (ends alone on its unresolved A7(b9) under Angelo's drain), M06b (the next record: A minor, 7/8, no G natural). Memory: M08a (the strings' D minor 9 as the hands join) and M10d (the Rhodes' single Dm9 at the jacket). Cadence: M11a (D minor stepping down D-C-B-flat into the A7(b9), ended before the spark).
- **THE LAMENT BASS (the house retuned)**: In the kitchen the bass sinks a semitone with each loss, following the old synth dread roots (55, 51.9 and 49 Hz) an octave up, until the house sits on the Extra's pitch class. Each time it is a single held low note, never a moving line. *Where it plays:* M10a: A2 under the stove struggle. M10b: G-sharp2 on one cello for Deena, stopping with her heart. M10c: G2 under the shaking knife tremolo. M10d: G2 under the aftermath's numb A-C-E.
- **HIS NOTE (the 7th harmonic)**: The Extra's only musical identity is the drone's natural 7th (384.7 / 385.4 Hz over 55 Hz, about 31 cents flat of G4), and it swells only when he acts. The score leaves it to drone7 and Q08.seventh. Lyria cannot play a G 31 cents flat, and an equal-tempered G4 would beat at about 7 Hz against his partial. So the score plays no G in the middle register and never covers his swells. It points at him only by displacement: the count's extra high G5, the high G5 at the top of Angelo's A7(b9), and the kitchen's bass sinking to G2. *Where it plays:* drone7 throughout (synth). Score gestures toward it: M01 and M12 (the sixth note), M11a (the top of the goodbye chord), M10c and M10d (the bass on G). The flat G is removed from every cue where the draft had it (its M02b, M05a, M05b, M07b, M08a, M08b, M09, M11b, M12a and M12b).

## Arc

ARC. The score is held back. It speaks only when a count comes out wrong, and it always gives way to the Extra (his drone, his holes). It builds by restraint:
- Underscore in Acts 1 to 3 is held dyads at pp to p, capped at mp.
- M08b is the first mf.
- The stove struggle (M10a) is the first and only time the score presses.
- The fire is scored only as grief.

COLD OPEN (0:00-0:32): the fire comes first. The score enters with the curtain on a cello's A3. Under 'six people' the felt piano counts six notes, and the sixth does not belong. The cue thins away by 25.6, leaving the ear ring, the caret's heartbeats and the smash cut (30.82) to the sound design.

ACT 1 (0:32-3:45):
- No Lyria score for the crisps, the glasses or the title. The film's own synth chord (Q02.warm) stays under the title and the credit 'A Film by Osita Miles'.
- The party is only its record: through the wall as the guests arrive (cut dead on the tally), in the room for the warmest scene, and through the doorway at the cards, cut dead on 'Angelo.'.

ACT 2 (3:45-7:09):
- The score's first word is a 3.4 s rub on the seventh pile, gone before the joke.
- It returns at Christof's pause and is cut with the room on 'Please.', so the six hands fall into the hole.
- The record creeps back, gets nothing on PERFECT, and ends alone on its unresolved A7(b9) under Angelo's drain.
- The next record is in the Extra's key and limps one beat too many in every bar, until the kitchen takes Christof.
- The crash, the bottle, the five glasses and 'Oh. Thanks.' play dry.
- In Kay's corridor one flat texture holds through the Christof logic. It steps once when they look at the bottle, holds on the doorway nobody will look at, and stops as Angelo reaches for the cards.

ACT 3 (7:09-9:34):
- The hand circle is one cue: a static A under the argument; the last warmth as the hands join, cut by hole 1; a still fifth under both roll calls; a held darkening in between.
- The fridge beat is the fridge alone.
- The first mf runs from the fifth wineglass to the let-go, and is cut dead.
- The photo gets cold stillness, with one change on 'Then let's go get them'. It is gone at the bathroom handle.

ACT 4, THE KITCHEN (9:34-11:26):
- The stove struggle presses, and the oven hole cuts it off.
- No score for the oven, the scream or the throat.
- One held G-sharp for Deena stops with her heart.
- A shaking low tremolo on G for the knife is cut with Marina.
- The aftermath is numb over G, with the party's Rhodes once at the jacket. It withdraws so the hiss gets louder.

ACT 5 (11:26-12:54): no score for the standoff; the hiss is its score. Angelo's goodbye steps from D minor into the unresolved A7(b9) and ends before the spark. Nothing for the run, the blast or the crawl.

CODA, TWO (12:54-14:54):
- Grief comes on the sparks: five falling cello notes.
- One numb cue carries across LATER and the call, ending in a static rub under the count until the hole for '6'.
- After the hit, the count returns and narrows to a bare A3 that goes out with the world.
- 'Me.' and 'Two.' are unscored. The unison takes that A, and the film ends on the unison and the locked hearts.

JOINS: no two cues overlap or crossfade. Every boundary is one of three things:
- a hard cut on a picture cut or an action: 143.92, 145.09, 191.09, 224.69, 280.97, 326.79, 389.79, 519.15, 631.05, 751.78, 839.35;
- one of the Extra's holes: M08a crosses 474.34 and 505.70; M09 crosses 547.60; M10a ends inside 605.80; M10c ends inside 644.05; M11b ends inside 838.60;
- designed silence.
The only butt joins are where the record changes on a time cut: 191.09 (M04a / M04b) and 326.79 (M06a / M06b).

BUDGET: 19 generations (15 lyria-3.5, 4 lyria-3-clip-preview). That leaves 3 retakes inside 22; M06b's 7/8 is the likeliest to need one.

- P6 (filters). source_filter carries only the low-pass the tool implements. Optional, once gain and filter automation exist:

- P7 (levels). The level_db values are starting points. Render the stem with and without --score and check:
  - each source cue sits about 3 to 6 LU above the synth groove it replaces;
  - each underscore cue sits clearly under the scene's bed over its span (about 6 LU under), except M10a;
  - M10a is at least 3 dB above every other cue;
  - the 100 Hz to 1 kHz band stays clear of the slaps and heartbeats.

## Kept synthesised

These are story devices rather than score, so they stay synthesised: drone7 (the Extra's leitmotif: A1 partials 1-6 plus the flat 7th at 384.7/385.4 Hz, on the 'extra' bus, never silenced; the score leaves the 7th to it), Q08.seventh (the 7th blooming under the VOICE words), unison (220 + 220.6 Hz, from 'Two.'; it takes the A that M12 leaves), ping (CU.ping, the souring count pings, including the Q02 glass pings and every wrong ping; the only souring in the film), tinnitus, Q01.tinn, Q01.ring (owns 24.3-30.82 into the smash cut), heart (all BD.heart beds, including Q12's second, locking heartbeat), Q07.heart, lub (single heartbeat cues), Q02.warm (the film's own in-tune D major chord under the title, 48.98-51.35, and under the credit 'A Film by Osita Miles', 52.30-55.13; no Lyria cue there).
