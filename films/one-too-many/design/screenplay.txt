FADE IN:


EXT. SCOTT'S BACK YARD - NIGHT

A HOUSE BURNS. Flames breathe through smashed windows. An
upstairs curtain lifts in the heat, then disappears.

SCOTT (late 20s) sits on the grass in his socks. His shirt
is torn. One arm is blistered. He holds a phone against an
ear that won't stop ringing.

                        DISPATCHER (V.O.)
           Sir? Stay with me. You said there
           were six people.

                       SCOTT
           Yeah.

                        DISPATCHER (V.O.)
           How many got out?

Scott stares at the house. Somewhere inside, glass breaks.

                       SCOTT
           I--

He can't finish.

                                                           24.

The blast throws Scott down the steps. Windows burst

scrambles, rolls again until the fire dies.

He crawls to the far fence, coughing and sobbing. A second
explosion sends sparks into the night.

For a while, there is nothing he can do but watch.
