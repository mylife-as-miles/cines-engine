# Sound credits

## Voices

Daniel's narration (12 lines) and the radio presenter (2 lines) are voiced with Google's Gemini text-to-speech (`gemini-3.8-flash-tts`), in British voices from Google's voice library: `en-gb-training-10` (Daniel) and `en-gb-podcaster-3` (the presenter), chosen by the author by ear (`design/DECISIONS.md`). The takes are in `audio/vo/` and `audio/radio/`.

## Score

15 cues generated by Google's Lyria 3.5 (`lyria-3.5`, `lyria-3-clip-preview`) through the Gemini API, from `audio/score_plan.json`. Output carries a SynthID watermark. The takes are in `audio/score/`.

## Effects and room tones

Generated locally with Stable Audio 3.0 Small SFX (Stability AI Community License), run through [audio.cpp](https://github.com/0xshug0/audio.cpp) and [Soundbox](https://github.com/HamSanders/soundbox): `audio/kit/` (from `audio/gen_plan.json`).

Designed tones synthesised in code (`tools/synth_sfx.py`): the phone alarm, the charging chime, the email, the laptop waking, someone else's ringtone, the flash cut, and (in the mix) the ringing and the heat's shimmer.
