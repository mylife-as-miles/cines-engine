# Aethelgard's tongues in the prologue

The in-world lines are spoken in the two languages of Aethelgard from the creator's *Ash & Aether Linguistic Atlas*
(`Crownfall_VR_The_Ash_and_Aether_Linguistic_Atlas.pdf`, Crownfall repository, `main`): its people speak **Old Aethel**
(a North-Ridge language with case endings and verb-second law speech), and its riders command the First-Born in **High
Drakonic** (a restricted rider-beast register whose verbs mark who acted: `-ar` rider, `-esh` beast, `-ae` both).
Subtitles are in English. The narrator and Cael speak English.

| Line | Speaker | Language | Spoken | Subtitle | Grammar and words (atlas) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| D01 | Adam (rider) | High Drakonic | *Varrath-ath vaha-esh! Searyn-esh!* | Down on the city! Burn! | High Drakonic (Linguistic Atlas): varrath 'territory' + -ath GOAL; vaha 'descend', searyn 'burn', both with the beast-agency ending -esh. The rider gives the order in the beast's agency, so in Aethelgard law the beast did it: the 'rookery disaster'. |
| A01 | Daniel (captain) | Old Aethel | *Tæsa, cyning.* | Forgive me, my king. | Old Aethel: tæsan 'forgive', singular imperative stem + -a; cyning 'king' (lowland speech merges y with i). |
| A02 | Charlie (guard) | Old Aethel | *Bleanda! Bleanda!* | Run! Run! | Old Aethel: bleandan 'run', singular imperative bleanda (go is suppletive, so it is avoided). |
| A03 | Bill (arbiter) | Old Aethel | *Dyld. Se cyning is dead.* | Mate. The king is dead. | Old Aethel: dyld 'mate' (Gambit lexicon); se cyning is dead, demonstrative se, copula is, dead 'dead'. |
| A04 | Brian (herald) | Old Aethel | *Risa, skand Ealdholtes.* | Rise, Knight of Ealdholt. | Old Aethel: risan 'rise', imperative risa; skand 'knight'; Ealdholt + genitive -es. |
| A05 | Callum (killer) | Old Aethel | *Wrardaþ. Nan ban.* | Burn him. Not one bone. | Old Aethel: wrardan 'burn', plural imperative -aþ (to his men); nan 'none', ban 'bone'. |

Pronunciation follows the atlas: Old Aethel stresses the first root syllable, keeps þ /θ/ and ð /ð/ apart and has
diphthongs ea [æɑ] and eo; lowland speech merges y with i (so *cyning* is spoken KIN-ing). High Drakonic holds rr, sings
long open vowels above the wind, and its y is central [ɨ]. The `say` field in `script.json` is the spelling the voice reads.

Story note: the Sorn rider's order uses the beast's agency ending (*vaha-esh*, *searyn-esh*), so under Aethelgard law the
First-Born did it. The atlas warns that a wrong actor ending can misattribute responsibility for a fatal beast attack; here it
is deliberate, and it is how the histories come to record a rookery disaster.
